### Chapter 1: The Gathering of Architects
In the quiet corners of the Imperial Conflict halls, a vacuum of power emerged. The moderators of old had laid down their pens, leaving the community in a state of suspended animation. The thread, titled "Next Mafia Host(s)," became a sanctuary for the ambitious—a crossroads where the future of the game was forged in the fires of speculation and ego.

### Chapter 2: The Pitch
The air grew thick with suggestions. Alundra and Ramza stepped forward, offering a partnership born of enthusiasm, their voices echoing with the whimsy of pony-themed aspirations. They warned of the complexities of the craft, demanding experience before the mantle of moderation could be donned.

### Chapter 3: The Contagion of Ambition
As the debate raged, others looked to seize the initiative. Meanwhile, the enigmatic Undeath offered a chilling counterpoint, suggesting that perhaps the community required a period of silence—a hibernation to recharge the collective spirit of the faction.

### Chapter 4: The Decree

The ambitions of the many were set aside for the partnership of the few.

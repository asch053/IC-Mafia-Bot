### Chapter 1: The Blueprint of Chaos
The atmosphere in the Imperial Conflict archives was thick with anticipation. Moderator Oxygen had cast a stone into the placid waters of tradition, proposing a game of unprecedented complexity: twenty-five souls, a labyrinth of secret roles, and a Serial Killer engineered to sow total discord. The proposal—an architectural masterpiece of paranoia—sent ripples through the community.

### Chapter 2: The Philosophers’ Debate
As the potential architects gathered, the discussion turned into a battlefield of ideologies. Arby3, a veteran of the "Modestus Experitus" school of thought, voiced a stern warning. He argued that the soul of Mafia lay in simplicity and trust; to over-engineer the roles was to invite confusion, turning the game into a "mind screw" rather than a test of social deduction. Poley echoed these fears, noting the toll such complexity would take on the casual player.

### Chapter 3: The Shadow of the Speedsters
Into this debate stepped TheBigOne, a proponent of radical brevity. He envisioned a "1-hour Mafia," a frantic, high-octane sprint where lives were discarded in minutes. This blasphemy against the long-form strategy sparked immediate friction. The moderators, represented by the swift and brutal hand of Arsy, maintained order with iron-fisted efficiency—kicking dissenters into the digital void before they could finish their sentences.

### Chapter 4: The Unresolved Legacy
In the end, the vision of the 25-player "Complex Mafia" remained a blueprint written in the stars, never manifesting as a singular, unified event. As the archives grew silent, the dream of the hyper-complex game dissolved into the ether, leaving behind only the ghost of an idea and the debate between the purists and the speed-addicts. The game concluded not with a lynch, but with a lingering stalemate of potentiality.

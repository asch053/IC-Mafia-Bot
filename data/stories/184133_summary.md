### Chapter 1: The Breach
The nightmare did not begin with a bang, but with a betrayal. When Jack, afflicted by a cocktail of disease and volatile rage, tore through his household, he sparked an infection that would bring the world to its knees. The city was placed under military quarantine, but the walls were thin, and the hunger of the infected was absolute. Within the claustrophobic confines of the compound, survivors gathered—some seeking salvation, others merely trying to survive the night. Among them walked the "Rages," monsters who had lost their humanity, and the "Venoms," carriers whose bodies had become vessels for a second, more virulent strain of the virus.

### Chapter 2: The Souring of the Soil
Death became the only constant. HydroP, the old farmer, fell first—cut down by Jack’s own wife, a woman consumed by a self-appointed mission to purge the infection. The paranoia within the quarantine zone reached a fever pitch. Citizens like Einstein and Keerb were sent to the gallows of the lynch mob, victims of suspicion rather than evidence. The military, led by the stern General Counterstrike, attempted to maintain order, but the command structure was rotting from within. The Fireman, a master of deception, moved through the shadows, while the Abandoned Soldier lurked in the corridors, nursing a grudge that turned lethal for anyone who dared cross his path.

### Chapter 3: Web of Deceit
The compound descended into true anarchy. The General, struggling to keep his unit together, was eventually cornered and butchered by the very monsters he sought to contain. Power shifted to Big Gary, a tyrant who sought to weaponize the virus rather than cure it. As the walls of the southern gate collapsed under the weight of a massive infected incursion, the lines between hero, victim, and monster blurred. The streets ran red; the elevator shafts became tombs; and the few remaining survivors found themselves hunted by their own neighbors, now transformed into hulking, mutated predators.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
In the end, the quarantine was not a fortress, but a cage. The surviving remnants of humanity, including Undeath and Banksy, attempted one last desperate rebellion against Big Gary. It was a suicide mission that ended in a symphony of gunfire and screams. As the smoke cleared, the hierarchy of the new world emerged. Skyroshroud, Genesis, and Testudinae—the ultimate victors—stood amidst the wreckage. The infection had claimed its prize; the "Rage" had won, leaving behind nothing but the hollow roars of a world that had forgotten how to be human.

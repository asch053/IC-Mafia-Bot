## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **[TI] arsbury***

As promised, IC Mafia is back!!The following are the characters in this game. These will be allocated at random to those who sign up (yes, my magical random spreadsheet is back!!):- 7 mafia members (1 Godfather, 1 corrupt Mayor, 5 mobsters)- 1 serial killer- 1 doctor- 2 police officers- 1 deputy mayor (pro-town)- 1 secret role (pro-town)- 1 secret role (alliance unknown)- 10 townsfolk= 24 playersOnly the Godfather and Mafia will know who the other mobsters are. Everyone else will be in the dark. --- NIGHT PHASE - WHO DOES WHAT? --- Each phase of the game begins with the night. The following can happen during this time:- Before the end of the first night, the Mafia must elect their godfather. The godfather is the leader of the Mafia. He, together with the other mobsters, will decide on person to kill each night. He will also choose one of them to conduct the kill. The Godfather has the final say in any Mafia night decision. He will also appear to be innocent to police investigations and is immune to being killed at night. If the Godfather is lynched, a new Godfather must be elected by the remaining mafia by the end of the following night.- The Corrupt Mayor has the ability to buy another town member's vote. This basically means to force one person to vote for someone else. By the end of the night phase, the Corrupt Mayor must specify the person he wants to bribe and who they should vote for. By the end of the day phase, that person MUST vote for who they are told, else they face being murdered.- The serial killer is a madman who kills one person every night. He works alone. He is immune to being killed at night and if investigated by the police, will turn up innocent. However, if the doctor chooses him to be healed, he will be cured and become an ordinary townie.- Doctor chooses one person to spare in the event that that person is chosen for death by the mafia families or serial killer. If the doctor tries to heal the SK, he will be cured and become an ordinary townsperson. The Doctor cannot heal himself and he is also forbidden to heal the same person two nights in a row.- Police officers can choose one person to investigate between them at night. They will then be told of this person's role. They can do what they like with this information but revealing it may make it obvious they are police and make them a target for the mafia. Note that investigating the Godfather or Serial Killer will return innocent results.- The Deputy Mayor counters the Corrupt Mayor by having two lynch votes in the Day phase. His first vote is made in the normal way, but his second vote is made in secret.- The pro-town secret role is secret!- The alliance unknown secret role is also secret. However, if this role is "activated", details of the role will be revealed to the town. This person is then immune to being killed at night. --- DAY PHASE - LYNCHING --- During the day phase, the town will find out who has been murdered. All townsfolk (including the Mafia, the serial killer, doctor, police) can then vote who they think are the mafia. This is the lynching process. There will be a deadline to which the people must vote by. If there is a draw, the deadline will be extended and voting will remain open for those who have tied. If there is still a draw by the new deadline, there will be no lynch. Once the result is in, the person will then be dead and their role will be revealed. Then that round is over and night begins again when the night roles make their next choices.All votes must be made in the forum in new posts (do not edit old posts). --- WHO WINS? --- The godfather and his Mafia will win if they manage to kill everyone else off. The serial killer wins if he is the last person alive and the rest win if they manage to kill off all the Mafia and the serial killer. --- RULES --- Everyone is allowed to post in the thread to discuss who they think is mafia or try to make themselves less suspicious (if they are Mafia!). You may NOT quote any part of messages sent by the game moderator. Doing so is regarded as cheating and will result in your sudden death.If any townsfolk miss the lynching voting on at most 2 occasions, they will be mod killed and replaced with a reserve. If no reserves are remaining, their role will be removed and revealed. Reserves will no longer be used after the 4th night.If you die, the game is over for you and you are not permitted to discuss the game with any of the people still playing. Doing so will result in a ban from future games. Thank you for turning up here at very short notice tonight. I have called this emergency town meeting with some news that will sho....." Darkness flooded the town hall and a sea of gasps made its way throughout the room.The sound of commotion grew as people stood up and cried out in shock and confusion. A baby starting crying. "Please be patient, all! We are in the process of getting the power back!" came a voice from the stage area. It had the distinct tone and accent of the Deputy Mayor of the town.After only a few moments of darkness, the electronic clunking sound of the backup generator was heard and power was restored. Light filled the room."Where's the Mayor?" a blonde lady sitting in the front row questioned.The crowd turned towards the front and saw the microphone stand soley on the stage. The black mic was swinging slowly back and forth on its wire. The Mayor was nowhere to be found. --- 2 hours later....Everything was a blur.The man tried to open his eyes again but struggled. The top of his head throbbed and the whole right side of his body was in constant pain. One of his eyes felt like it had grown to the size of a tennis ball but he still attempted to prise them open a couple of centimetres.Once the foggyness had cleared in his line of sight he saw that he was in a small room, no bigger than a wine cellar. The door was open to his right and the only light came from the fire crackling in the fireplace to his left. He mentally told his arms to move but he realised they were tied behind his back. His legs were tightly tied to the chair he was sitting on. Sat directly in front of him was a man who looked vaguely familiar, dressed in a black suit and playing around with his phone. The man looked up."Well well well - wakey wakey Mr Mayor!" he said with a wry smile on his face. "Guys! He's awake, get your asses down here. Bring the girl too!"A shuffle of footsteps was heard above as the other mobsters made their way into the cellar. Two of them were holding a fifteen year old girl, dragging her roughly down the stairs by the tops of her arms. Her legs had been tied and when she saw who was tied up in the chair in front of her, she began to sob uncontrollably into the tape that covered her mouth."That's right Mayor, she's been keeping my boys company" the man said as he ran a long boney finger along the girl's cheek. The Mayor struggled again to be free of his bindings and cried out, but he was trapped and only a faint murmur came out of his bloated lips. "If you want your girl unharmed, you'll do whatever I say. You got that Mayor?"The Mayor exerted all the energy he had left to attempt to break free but only resulted in moving the chair forwards slightly. The man reacted angrily and slapped the girl across the face. She whimpered in pain and hung her head, blood dripping from her nose. "STOP WASTING MY TIME!" he shouted and walked towards fireplace where a branding iron was rested. The man picked it up and inspected the end of the iron. It was glowing red as if it had been sitting in the fire for hours.The Mayor looked on, unable to speak and afraid of what might happen next. His captives unbuttoned the lowest two buttons of his daughter's shirt to reveal her navel. She lifted her head slightly and kicked out as saw the man approach with the brand. She had already experienced that pain once today. But the mobsters holding onto her only tightened their grip. There was a sizzling sound as the red hot brand touched the bare skin on her stomach and the girl retracted in agony.The stale stench of his girl's burning flesh was too much. He couldn't take anymore. The Mayor uttered the words "I'll do it".The Godfather smiled and said "You're one of us now, Mayor" as the corrupted Mayor looked up and nodded slowly. "This town will be ours again my brothers!" ---

He fondly looked again at the photo on his mantlepiece.Taken a few years previously, the photo showed a sunburnt Gwynedd and Wendy cuddling in some exotic destination on their honeymoon, big smiles beaming on their faces, without a care in the world.

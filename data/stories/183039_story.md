Imagine Nolio finding Doctor Watson and using his skills to keep Undeath from investigating mob... lol. I still need to think a good balance out here if ever I mod again and mafia is to get investigations.6) Invincible roles and a story line based upon themI tried to change the focus of the game to the dynamics of good versus evil than individual players and their actions.

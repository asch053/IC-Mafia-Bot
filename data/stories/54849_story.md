*Original opening by Moderator **[TI] arsbury***

Mafia's back and we're going back to basics!

As usual, the following are the list of characters. These will be allocated to players who sign up randomly using the Excel rand() function!7 Mobsters (including a Mafia elected Godfather)1 Pro-Mafia secret role2 IBI Agents (Imperial Bureau of Investigations)2 GIA Agents (Galactic Intelligence Agency)1 Jail keeper1 Serial Killer12 TowniesOnly the godfather and Mafia will know who the other mobsters are. Everyone else will be in the dark.NIGHT PHASE
- WHO DOES WHAT?

Each phase of the game begins with the night. The following can happen during this time:- Before the end of the first night, the Mafia must elect their godfather. The godfather is the leader of the Mafia. He, together with the other mobsters, will decide on person to kill each night (this is also possible if he is in jail). He will also choose one of them to conduct the kill. The godfather has the final say in any Mafia night decision. He will also appear to be innocent to IBI investigations and is immune to being killed at night.
- The Mafia's secret role has powers hidden to everyone.
- The Imperial Bureau of Investigations (IBI) agents are a special branch of the government who specialise in conducting secret investigations for mobsters. Two undercover agents have been assigned to this case. They know each other and decide between them each night to investigate one person. As a result, they will learn this person's role. They can do what they like with this information but revealing it might reveal their identities.
- Galactic Intelligence Agency (GIA) agents are also from a special branch of the government. However, their actions are highly confidential and their work is top secret.
- The jail keeper holds the keys to the jail. The jail is housed in a secure part of town. The person who is sent to jail is safe from harm until they are released the next morning. As long as the jail keeper lives, all town residents are able to vote to put someone in jail. This vote takes place during the day phase together with the lynch vote. This person is locked in, which makes him/her protected during the night phase, but it also disables his nightly activities (apart from the godfather, who is still able to instruct his mobsters who to kill). When the jail keeper dies, the jail becomes unavailable. Since it's his responsibility, he holds 2 votes in the vote on who to send to jail. His second vote will be made in secret and posted on his behalf by the game mods. It is impossible to put the same person in jail two nights in a row. The jail keeper is unable to vote for himself to enter the jail. On the first night only, the jail keeper locks himself away behind the bars.
- The serial killer is a madman who kills one person every night. He is immune to being killed at night but if placed in the jail, he will commit suicide.DAY PHASE
- LYNCHINGDuring the day phase, everyone in the town will find out who has been murdered. All townsfolk (including the Mafia, the serial killer, jail keeper, IBI and GIA agents) can then vote who they think are mobsters. This is the lynching process. There will be a deadline to which the people must vote by. If there is a draw, the deadline will be extended and voting will remain open for everyone. If there is still a draw by the new deadline, all of those with the joint highest votes will be lynched. Once the result is in, the person will then be dead and their role will be revealed. Then that round is over and night begins again when the night roles make their next choices.

In addition to the lynch vote, all townsfolk must also vote on who to send to jail during the coming night phase. More details are above.

This means that townsfolk should cast 2 votes during each day phase - one for lynch and one for the jail.WHO WINS?

The godfather and his Mafia will win if they manage to kill everyone else off. The serial killer wins if he is the last person alive and the rest win if they manage to kill off all the Mafia and kill the serial killer.

Everyone is allowed to post in the thread to discuss who they think is infected or try to make themselves less suspicious (if they are Mafia!). You may NOT quote any part of messages sent by the game moderators. Doing so will result in your sudden death. Also, when you die you are dead and are no longer part of the game, you are not allowed to discuss the game with anyone.SIGN UPFor those who want to play, please in game Ruffles in #1735 with a message saying who you are and that you want to play Mafia. - A tall man with dark slick hair was running through the narrow back alleys of Brondane. Tightly gripping an old rusty revolver in his left hand, he panted heavily as he manourved around the trash cans which lined his route. His other hand was holding a piece of red stained cloth which he used to try to stop the bleeding from his left side.

He paused for a moment to look where he could go next and noticed a small drug store on the corner of Mercury Street. He quickly rammed the lock and entered the small store looking for something to stop the pain and bleeding. While rummaging through the supplies, the sudden sound of the little bell that rang whenever somebody opened the front door caught his ear. Silently he took cover behind one of the shelves whilst readying his revolver.

By then three mobsters had entered the drug store, one of them seemingly the leader of the group. The leader spoke up loudly as the others raised their pistols."We know you are in here Jimmy or should I say Special Agent Vincent McCallister..?YOU THINK YOU CAN FOOL ME?

Who the hell do you think you are, coming to my house, pretending to be my friend?NO ONE MESSES WITH THE FAMILY!!"The leader nodded and the mobsters started to look for the IBI agent. They checked shelf by shelf as they went deeper into the store. They reached the last possible place where the agent could be hiding and they steadied their weapons. Both mobsters jumped around the corner pointing at.. nothing! There was nobody there!

Meanwhile Jimmy had slipped past the two mobsters and was now standing behind the Godfather. He placed the barrel of his revolver against the back of the head and whispered: "Game over, Mafian scum bag!" He pulled the trigger and splattered the brains of the mastermind of the Lamiolo crime family against the wall.

The two mobsters span around to see their leader collapse to the ground. In a daze, they raised their weapons towards Jimmy and emptied their barrels. Bullet after bullet pierced his body as he slowly dropped to the floor. He felt his life was flowing away and the last thing he heard were the fading footsteps of the two mobsters fleeing the scene. --- 49 hours later

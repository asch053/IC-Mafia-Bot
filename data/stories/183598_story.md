The storyM sat back in her chair gazing at the files strewn about her desk and sighed. Damn 007! Looking at the file a moment she dropped it onto the desk in front of 008 with a thump."Your new case, 008. Last week we received word that two nuclear weapons were stolen from a secure base in Russia. We just got news of a rumor that they were taken by agents of SPECTRE. I need you to confirm SPECTRE involvement and take whatever steps are needed to recover the weapons. It goes without saying that whatever SPECTRE is up to needs to be stopped.”008 took a moment to flip through the folder and crooked an eyebrow at M. “I thought James had first shot on all SPECTER cases?”“007 is not available at this time, though should he become available I have every intention of sending him in as well, “ M glared. “Any questions?”“No ma’am.”“Very well, 008. Godspeed.”M watched 008 leave the room and rubbed her temples. This was going to be an interesting case. --

When the weapons are in place we will start issuing our demands. The world will never be the same when we are through. Dismissed!” Blofeld watched his agents leave. After the last had left and the door was closed a soft but harsh chuckle could be heard filling the room. This was going to be so much fun.

- Number 1 sat behind his cup of wine. The gentle clinking of fine china and silverware settling around him like leaves from a tree. Number 1 hid behind his cup of wine and watched his prey. His prey. He liked that. The other SPECTRE agents were out tonight, busy with their tasks, but this thread of suspicion had cropped up in the locals, and he was going to put a stop to it tonight. A few judicious deaths and they would have too much on their hands to worry about the activities of his group of compatriots.

After a time his prey stood and made his way to the back of the resturant and Number 1 followed silently. Entering the restroom after, his prey had enough time to turn around and see his face before Number 1 put one bullet through his forehead, replaced his silenced gun into his holster and left the restroom and building.

Back at headquarters, Number 1 sat behind his desk, and pet his cat."One less nosy local, my dear, who should have known to keep his mouth shut."Number 1 punctuated his statement with another stroke of the fine white fur and gave the cat a ghastly grin. --

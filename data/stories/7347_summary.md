### Chapter 1: The Gathering Storm
The year is 3000. The geopolitical map of the world has been rewritten; China, the master of global finance, holds the strings of the world, while a debt-ridden United States lurks in the shadows as a fallen warmonger. But beneath the veneer of development, the fires of insurrection burn. As the superpowers prepare to clash, one man watches from the northern peninsula, hungry for the apocalypse: Kim Jong-un. North Korea, the self-proclaimed future Supreme Leader, prepares his nuclear arsenal, hoping that the titans will annihilate each other, leaving only him to claim the radioactive crown.

### Chapter 2: Web of Deceit
The shadows of the UN headquarters grew long. Japan, under the guise of an ally, played a treacherous game, using stealth to paralyze the movements of nations. Meanwhile, the Vatican began to stir. Saint Michael, the harbinger of a divine purge, watched as the world succumbed to corruption. The tension was palpable—China’s economic reach tried to stifle dissent, while the Mob, led by a sneaky and protected GodFather, moved in the dark, coordinating strikes across the globe.

### Chapter 3: The Betrayal
Diplomacy collapsed in spectacular fashion. Japan attempted to seize the initiative with a grand speech at the UN, unaware that his isolation had left his homeland vulnerable. While Japan spoke, his island nation was erased by a sudden, catastrophic nuclear strike. Bereft of his homeland, the Japanese representative fled to France, renouncing his citizenship to survive the dying world. But the chaos was only beginning. The Vatican, guided by a wrathful deity, turned its sights upon the world, wiping out minor powers and watching as the great nations bled each other dry.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
In the final hours, the grand ambition of the superpowers turned to ash. With China crippled by the loss of its economic heart and Vietnam’s defensive posturing failing to stop the carnage, the world order shattered. North Korea and the Vatican emerged as the final architects of the new age. With the UN dissolved and the French surrendering in the face of inevitable destruction, the world was unified under an iron grip. South Korea’s leadership was executed, and the "World War 3000" concluded not with a victory for the titans, but with the total ascendancy of the Serial Killer and the Divine Purge.

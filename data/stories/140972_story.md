## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **[TI] arsbury***

Woohoo! Hot on the heels of the townie win in the mafia's return, we bring to you IC Mafia 26!With the help of Ben and Torqez, we aim to get started this weekend. This means sign ups are NOW OPEN! Keep checking back on this thread as the role list and rules will be confirmed during the course of today.The following are the characters in this game. These will be allocated at random to those who sign up (yes, my magical random spreadsheet is back!!):- 8 mafia members split across 2 opposing families- family A (1 Godfather, 1 secret role, 2 regular mobsters)- family B (1 Godfather, 1 Secret role, 2 regular mobsters)- 1 serial killer- 1 doctor- 2 police officers (1 investigator, 1 watcher)- 2 secret pro town roles- 9 townsfolk= 23 playersThe two mafia families will know who else is in their family. Everyone else will be in the dark. --- NIGHT PHASE - WHO DOES WHAT? --- Each phase of the game begins with the night. The following can happen during this time:- Before the end of the first night, each Mafia family must elect their godfather. The godfather is the leader of their respective families. He, together with his fam members, will decide on one person to kill each night. He will also choose one of them to conduct the kill. The Godfather has the final say in the night decision for his family. He will also be innocent to police investigation and is immune to being killed at night. If the godfather is lynched, he must choose his successor by the end of the following night.- The mafia secret roles in each family have the same special abilities... which are secret!- The serial killer is a madman who kills one person every night. He works alone. He can't be killed at night but can be lynched during the day. The doctor is unable to cure him.- Doctor chooses one person to spare in the event that that person is chosen for death by the mafia families or serial killer. The Doctor cannot heal himself and he is also forbidden to heal the same person two nights in a row. Healing the serial killer does not cure him.- There are two police officers, each has a special ability. The investigator carries out an investigation on their target to find out whether they are aligned to the town, mafia fam A or mafia fam B. The watcher keeps a watch on a chosen person all night and can see any people who visited that person during the night. The police work together, but they may only use one of their abilities per night. They can do what they like with this information but revealing it may make it obvious they are police and make them a target for the mafia. Also note that investigating either of the godfathers or SK will incorrectly show that he is aligned with the town.- The 2 pro-town secret roles are secret! --- DAY PHASE - LYNCHING --- During the day phase, the town will find out who has been murdered. All townsfolk (including the Mafia, the serial killer, doctor, police) can then vote who they think are the mafia. This is the lynching process. There will be a deadline to which the people must vote by. If there is a draw, the deadline will be extended and voting will remain open for those who have tied. If there is still a draw by the new deadline, there will be no lynch. Once the result is in, the person will then be dead and their role will be revealed. Then that round is over and night begins again when the night roles make their next choices.All votes must be made in the forum in new posts (do not edit old posts). --- WHO WINS? --- Either mafia family will win if they manage to kill off everyone else including the rival family. The serial killer wins if he is the last person alive and the rest win if they manage to kill off both Mafia families and the serial killer. --- RULES --- Everyone is allowed to post in the thread to discuss who they think is mafia or try to make themselves less suspicious (if they are Mafia!). You may NOT quote any part of messages sent by the game moderator. Doing so is regarded as cheating and will result in your sudden death.If any townsfolk miss the lynching voting on at most 2 occasions, they will be mod killed and replaced with a reserve. If no reserves are remaining, their role will be removed and revealed. Reserves will no longer be used after the 4th night.If you die, the game is over for you and you are not permitted to discuss the game with any of the people still playing. Doing so will result in a ban from future games. Please don't spoil it for everyone else. --- SIGN UP --- For those who want to play, you can do this by ingaming Mayor in #5080 or emailing[email protected]and say who you are and that you want to play mafia.Deadline to do this is 20:00 UK time on Saturday 9th July.Once the deadline is reached, I will use a random Excel formula to pick our players and will publish the list below. Role descriptions will be sent out shortly after and we'll begin!Again, you cannot sign up if you are forum banned and I will be using the last game to judge whether you're IRC active enough to play! --- FINAL PLAYER LIST AND ROLES ***1.

### Chapter 1: The Gathering at Lake McConaughy
The summer of 1963 was supposed to be a time of simple pleasures—cold beer, the scent of fresh pine in new cabins, and the quiet pride of a nation looking toward the stars. The reunion in Ogallala was meant to be a family affair, a bridge between the dusty fields of Nebraska and the soaring ambition of the Houston space program. But as the sun dipped behind the dam, the tranquility of the lake was shattered. What began as a celebration of kin quickly soured into a waking nightmare.

### Chapter 2: The Serpent in the Grass
The first body found under the moonlight wasn't just a tragic drowning; it was a omen. By the time the sheriff’s cruiser peeled away from the lakeside cabins, the realization dawned on the family: someone amongst them was carving out a space for cold-blooded murder. The accident at the bluffs was far too convenient, the "drowning" far too calculated. The family tree was being pruned, and the roots were stained with blood.

### Chapter 3: The Fractured Alliance
Suspicion turned the reunions into a crucible of paranoia. Every cousin, every uncle, and every distant relative became a potential predator. When the group gathered to cast their judgments, the mob mentality took hold. ZoZ was the first to fall under the weight of the collective gaze—an ordinary man accused of extraordinary sins, dragged out and cast aside as the family desperately tried to purge the rot.

### Chapter 4: The Silent Fade
The end of the story is whispered rather than told. Rizzy, perhaps overcome by the suffocating tension or perhaps harboring secrets too heavy to carry, simply ceased to participate. When the moderator’s gavel finally fell, the family was left shattered. The truth of the Houston connection and the secrets of the space race remained buried in the mud of the lake, leaving only the cold wind of the prairie to howl over the empty cabins.

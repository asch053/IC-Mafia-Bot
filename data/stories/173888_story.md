## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **[TI] Primo***

IC Mafia pirate fest!Arrrr mateys! As we hoped, Pirateday be back! And its hidden himself as IC MafiaDue to the fact that Arsy is currently travelling along with the Justin Bieber world wide tour, Iluvatar and I have taken the responsibility of hosting this game.The lack of the Arsyman however means we will not be able to use the old forums. Mafia will have a new forum, other roles can use ingame msg's or irc to contact the mods or have discussions.The following are the characters in this game. These will be allocated at random :- 4 Mutineers (1 leader, 3 mutineers)- 1 Hyperthermic sailor- 1 Surgeon (pro-town)- 1 Quartermaster (pro-town)- 9 crewmembers= 16 playersOnly the mutineers will know who the other mutineers are. Everyone else will be in the dark.The game starts during the day phase. --- DAY PHASE - LYNCHING --- During the day phase, the town will find out who has been murdered (not during the first day, obviously). All pirates (including the mutineers, the hyperthermic sailor , the surgeon and the quartermaster) can then vote who they think are the mutineers. This is the lynching process. There will be a deadline to which the people must vote by. In the event of a draw, the lynching will be postponed by a couple of hours, allowing people to change their vote. If after the second deadline there still is a draw, there will be no lynching. Once the result are in, the chosen pirate will be keelhauled to death and their role will be revealed. The day-phase is over and the night phase begins.All votes must be made in the forum in new posts (do not edit old posts). --- NIGHT PHASE - WHO DOES WHAT? --- During the night phase the following happens:- The mutineers will decide on who to kill, and who will perform the kill. The leader of the mutineers has the final say in this, and has the responsibility to notify the game-moderators of the decision. Please both mention the target and the assassin.- The surgeon will choose a target whom he will protect. If the pirate chosen by the doctor is targeted by the mutineers or the hyperthermic sailor, he will be saved. If the surgeon targets the hyperthermic sailor he will be cured and become a loyal pirate.- The quartermaster will choose one pirate to be confined to their quarters. They will be roleblocked, but at the same time they will be immune to being killed.The hyperthermic sailor has gone crazy over exposure to the sun and will attempt to kill one pirate every night phase. --- WHO WINS? --- The mutineers will win if they manage to kill everyone else off. The hyperthermic sailor wins if he is the last person alive, and the loyal pirates win if they manage to kill off all the mutineers and kill or heal hyperthermic sailor. --- RULES --- Everyone is allowed to post in the thread to discuss who they think are the mutineers or try to make themselves less suspicious. You may NOT quote any part of messages sent by the game moderators, either ingame, in e-mail or in IRC. Doing so is regarded as cheating and will result in your sudden death, and you will be banned from future games.If anyone misses the lynching voting on 2 occasions, they will automatically be the next lynch-target.If you die, the game is over for you and you are not permitted to discuss the game with any of the people still playing. Doing so will result in a ban from future games. Please don't spoil it for everyone else. --- FINAL PLAYER LIST --- 1. Einstein2. Genesis3. Gwynedd4. Arby35. Wendy6. Ramza7. Thirdrock8. KT9. Twisted Metal10. Ps11. Oxygen12. Airwing13. Elrohir14. RisingDown15. lamb16. Nolio17. Portgas- For a starting date I will consult with Iluvatar.- Until that time I will allow further sign ups.- Roles will be sent out as soon as we know a starting date.NEE NAW NEE NAWPrimo

## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **Elrohir***

Pinwheel, Planet Arsylia, Capitol CityThree elderly gentlemen were sipping tea and eating biscuits at Cafe Guillotin's terrace near the town square. Their appearance was of nobility and wisdom. They talked calmly of things that had happened in their formerly small town had that grown into a metropolitan at an incredible speed. With econimical growth, the downsides had also appeared. The city had seen a huge rise in organised crime, chiefly the group known as Arsylian Mafia. As the men were getting ready to order more biscuits, a black transport flew past them at a hurried pace. The men stopped talking as they watched it go. It quickly vanished between the skyscrapers."There goes another prisoner transport to the portal. I wonder if it will ever end", spoke one of the men."It should encourage others not to seek the criminal way of life, bishop", replied a man next to him who looked slightly doggish. "And it's a one-way trip through the portal, to another galaxy. The prison planets are foolproof and there's no coming back from them."Sagittarius Dwarf, System 55,46, Prison planet 7 "Rae"Rae was a secluded prison planet that had been put into use as more and more industrialised planets had begun to struggle with rising prisoner populations. The planet had a special oneway portal that worked only one way: You could get onto the planet, but not away from it. As maximum security prisons go, a designated planet for prisoners was the best anyone had come up with so far. No guards, no upkeep costs. As one might guess, the planet's population was less than pleasant: Murderers, sexual deviants, thieves, televangelists, mobsters, and so forth. But still the convicts had installed rudimentary signs of civilisation. They had built lodgings and they even had a makeshift court that was rarely used as there was really not much law to speak of. Everything was ran with principles of anarchy.Convicts watched as the portal begun to glow blue again as a sign of incoming travellers. They were suspicious, even more so than usual. Recently many of the new prisoners were suspected of being members of organised crime, and there were rumours that the Arsylian Mafia wanted to enslave the other prisoners as their servants. For the first time in a long while the court system had been put to use. Every day they'd gather by the portal and vote for the execution of someone who they thought was a mobster. Of course, there was no saying how the Mob would react to this. Tt was likely they'd respond with violence and skip their original plan of enslaving the regular convicts.When the new arrivals came through the portal, they were quickly beaten and their few possessions were stolen. "Welcome to Rae", grinned one of the convicts as he was pulling off a newcomers' shoes. --- As usual, the following are the list of characters. The following can happen during this time:- Before the end of the first night, the Mafia must elect their godfather. The godfather is the leader of the Mafia. Like the Godfather, he is immune to being killed/cured at night and will only die if lynched.- The Martyr is an heroic madman who has the ability to kill one person once in the game. Unfortunately the martyr will not survive an attack on the Mafia.- The snitches have the power to investigate one inmate per night. By daytime, he will discover this person's role. He can do what he likes with this information but revealing it might reveal his identity. (formerly known as the cops)- The Dealer has the ability to save one person per night. That person will be protected from any possible attack by the Mafia or serial killer. The dealer is forbidden from choosing the same person two nights in a row and he cannot choose himself. (formerly known as the doctor)- The two lovers are so in love that they share the same fate. Might one die, the other will commit suicide. Obviously they will know who their lover is, but won't know their lover's role. (This love connection can be a connection between any two kinds of roles). Their ultimate goal is to survive together. If they are left over together then they win, whatever role they both have.- The Elder of Rae - is an additional role, which means it is on top of your given role. The elder has two votes in every lynching and is elected by the town on day 1. Upon death, the elder chooses his or her predecessor. (formerly known as the mayor)DAY PHASE - LYNCHINGDuring the day phase, everyone in the town will find out who has been murdered. All townsfolk (including the mafia, the serial killer, dealer and snitches) can then vote who they think are mobsters. This is the lynching process. There will be a deadline to which the people must vote by. If there is a draw, the deadline will be extended and only those drawing will be voted on. If there is still a draw by the new deadline, there will be no lynching. Once the result is in, the person will then be dead and their role will be revealed. Then that round is over and night begins again when the night roles make their next choices.WHO WINS?The godfather and his Mafia will win if they manage to kill everyone else off. ### 🌙 Night / Day Narrative Scene (14-Oct-2009 10:21:15)

Prisoner number 11. Torqez. Sentenced for poaching, hunted kiwis.Torqez slept uneasily in his hovel made of kangaroo dung. It had taken him quite a while to find enough on this planet were kangaroos were rare animals, but his homesickness had prevailed and he had spent months gathering the kangaroo manure. The smell of kangaroos reminded him of his wife who he hadn't seen since he had been sent to Rae. Little did he know that outside his hovel, mobsters had gathered. They carefully removed the pieces of wood that held the hovel together. When the structure was weakened, one of the mobsters gave the hovel a good kick. It collapsed, sending in air fumes of old kangaroo dung. Torqez didn't even have time to wake up. He was buried under kangaroo dung and choked to death. Mafia had drawn first blood: Torqez was a regular townie. --- Prisoner number 3, Arfeh. Sentenced for treason, called queen mother of his home planet "an old harlot".Arfeh was in his tent sharpening pointy sticks. He was planning on settling some old scores. Disturbed mumbling could be heard from his tent: "Dastardly Nolio, I'll have his head on a pole in a jiffy. Next one's Arnor. Teeheehee. And then I'll have crumpets. For king and country!" A lone character approached his tent. The serial killer had chosen its victim for the night. When Arfeh stepped out of his tent, the serial killer knocked him down with a quick blow.When Arfeh came to it, he found himself naked in a hole in the ground with his hands and legs tied together. There was also a sweet smell in the air. He looked down and saw that his groin area had been covered in honey. Just when he was wondering if he was going to be a victim of especially kinky prison rape, he saw a bag appear at the top of the hole. The bag was kicked down and swarms of rats ran out of it.Serial killer ran away humming a merry tune. In the distance, he could hear Arfeh scream as the rats started enjoying their nightly meal. Another regular convict had perished.***Morning come, the community of convicts found one of them buried under a pile of dung and another dead in a hole swarming with fat rats. They quickly organised a barbeque. Rarely had they seen such well-fed rats on this planet. --- It is now ** DAY ** phase. ### 🌙 Night / Day Narrative Scene (14-Oct-2009 22:26:37)

Prisoner number 15, melvin. Sentenced for thievery.Melvin wasn't exactly one of the toughest criminals on the planet. He had actually been a performing artist of sorts. He had been a mirror-eater. People would bring him mirrors and pay to eat them. It was a show, and business was good. Soon enough, other thieves were stealing mirrors for the show on his home world. When people tried to make reports, the police laughed and threw the reporters out. Surely no one would be idiotic enough to steal just plain mirrors and nothing else. However, the law took an interest when they found even their own police station mirrorless. Finally melvin became megalomaniac and turned himself in. He was convinced the planetary lord of his planet would be interested to see him eat mirrors. The police disagreed, locked him up and a court hearing was held. Sentenced for thievery and sent to Rae.Now on Rae, melvin was standing amongst other convicts near the portal when the daily lynching votes were read. It quickly became obvious he'd be the one to be terminated. Crowd was shouting suggestions:"Kill him and check his pockets for mafia stuff?""Couldn't we check the pockets first? Seems kind of stupid to kill him first and check his pockets after."And so they did. They checked his pockets and found nothing but a lonely hand mirror. But they weren't convinced. "Kill him anyway, just to be sure!" Now the crowd was shouting suggestions on how to kill him."Crush his balls!""Rape him!""Drown him in bukkake!""Death by snu snu!"Melvin laughed: "You be retarded, all you are. I never see anybody raped to death."Forward stepped Nolio, with a small cap on the back of his head. He said: "You will now. ### 🗳️ Vote Tally & Lynch Announcement (14-Oct-2009 23:24:01)

Prisoner number 7, shlimy. Sentenced for inbreeding.Shlimy had spent his youth on a farm planet, growing food crops with his family. And as was customary on his planet, at the age of 19 he wed his sister and they had children. They had many children and lived happily. However their happiness was shortlived. From the governmental center of the planet came a new law: Marrying one's close relatives was illegal. And it worked retroactively. Understandably a lot of people on the planet were enraged, but it couldn't be helped. In one night, the planet turned out to look like it had been ravaged by a series of electric storms, so quick was the disappearance of population. More than 80% of the population of the redneck planet were sent to different prisoner planets through portal, for the crime of inbreeding. Amongst them was Shlimy.On Rae, Shlimy had lived a quiet life. Happy to grow his few crops and milk his starved cow. He did not understand this commotion about mafias and regular convicts and serial killers and whatnot. He was a simple farmboy, such things did not mean much to him.The dim night that claimed his life was like any other he had spent in jail. He was in his small barn milking the cow for the last day today, and after that he'd get to bed to get well rested for tomorrow's chores. He rose from the stool he had been sitting on and grabbed the milk bucket. He was just pondering on the miracle of milk when he felt a piano wire tighten around his neck. A few weak struggles and strong tugs later Shlimy was dead and his cow was mooing sadly at the untimely death of its owner. Another regular convict had died.The mobster looked around the barn. "Huh", he thought and looked at the cow. Quickly he positioned the piano wire around the cow's neck and started pulling. "Better safe than sorry!" --- Prisoner number 19, Elbekko. Sentenced for downloading copyrighted material.Considered one of the most heinous crimes in the galaxy, Elbekko had been accidentally found to possess illegal copies of movies and games when the policemen were doing a routine home search for drugs. The sentence for this crime comparable to childkilling was guessable: Prison planet. Rae.During his sentence, Elbekko had built a makeshift computer in his hovel. It wasn't much to look at, hole cards and strings. But it gave him comfort to know that even in this hellhole, he could do rudimentary programming tasks and feel at least a bit at home.That night Elbekko was once again working on his computer. He was just reaching for a new hole card when he felt a cloth on his face and smelled chloroform. His limp body fell to the floor. The serial killer let out a maniac laughter, positioned Elbekko next to the wall and crucified him. Elbekko woke up screaming when the serial killer sliced his penis right off. Still screaming, the regular convict Elbekko saw the serial killer leave through the door, but not before letting in two pitbulls. Even through his pain his analytical mind was working: For the brief moment it took the dogs to reach him and start eating, he wondered where someone would get pitbulls on this planet. --- Morning broke. The prisoners found a strangled Shlimy, a strangled cow, a mutilated Elbekko and two fat pitbulls. Again they arranged a barbeque of one cow and two dogs, meat was not to be wasted. But in their gluttony, they couldn't help but feel a chill: Five regular townies were dead. Was there any chance of defeating the notorious Arsylian Mafia? Or the mad serial killer? --- IT IS NOW ** DAY ** phase.

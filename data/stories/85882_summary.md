### Chapter 1: The Gathering of Archives
The atmosphere in the Imperial Conflict chambers was thick, not with the scent of gunpowder or the sting of betrayal, but with the dust of forgotten ledgers. Moderator K, acting as a curator of the realm's fading memory, emerged from the void with a plea—a call to reconstruct the lost history of the community. It was a search for truth, a quest to tally the fallen and codify the secret roles that had once shaped the destiny of the Empire.

### Chapter 2: The Inquiry
The call was answered by the Chronicler Elrohir and the vigilant [RPA] Arocalex. No daggers were drawn; no lynch mobs formed under the moonlight. Instead, a debate sparked over the fragility of record-keeping. Elrohir lamented the loss of Arsy’s ancient statistical scrolls, while Arocalex pondered the mathematical impossibility of reconstruction without the raw data of the fallen. They stood at the precipice of history, realizing that the records were not merely incomplete—they were effectively ghosts, leaving the "Mafia in Review" as an unfinished epitaph in the annals of the game.

### Chapter 3: The Silent Void
As the ink dried on the final post, the realization settled over the chamber: the game had dissolved into a quiet administrative uncertainty. Without a definitive end, the story of this review remains an open-ended mystery. The history was not written in blood, but in the echoes of a community trying to remember who they once were, leaving the final tally to the whispers of the archives.

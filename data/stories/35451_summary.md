### Chapter 1: The Glitch in Earth 2.0
The city of Earth 2.0 lived in the shadow of the Great Electric Storm, a utopia built on the backs of synthetic "humanoids." But the dream fractured when President arsbury announced a terrifying truth: the latest batch of humanoids had been compromised. Their control cards were not just faulty; they had been hacked. Amidst the panic of a city that could no longer distinguish neighbor from machine, a clandestine war began. Beneath the neon glow of holographic billboards, the Mad Scientist and his legion of hacked humanoids prepared to purge the city, while a lone Serial Killer lurked in the dark, seeking total dominion.

### Chapter 2: First Blood
The first night was marred by hesitation and silent betrayal. While the Medic attempted to safeguard the populace, their efforts were largely in vain against the cold, calculated efficiency of the hacked collective. As the sun rose on the City Square, the people demanded a sacrifice to quell their fear. They turned their suspicion toward Arnor. In a brutal display of authority, the Moderator activated a remote deactivator, freezing Arnor in place before executing him with a blast of white light. To the crowd's horror, the autopsy revealed a control panel in his neck—but he was merely an ordinary humanoid. The city had spilled the blood of an innocent.

### Chapter 3: Web of Deceit
The game of shadows intensified. The elusive Serial Killer, You_Fool, found himself hunted by the very forces he sought to destroy, eventually falling to the collective’s reach. Meanwhile, the humble chef Maarten witnessed the true face of the enemy in his own kitchen. Struck down by a hacked humanoid, he experienced a miraculous, flickering survival—only for his luck to run dry as the Mafia tightened their grip. A lethal bomb, passed from hand to hand like a deadly game of hot potato, finally detonated in the hands of The_Unknown, shattering any hope of organized resistance.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
The endgame unfolded in the City Square. With their numbers dwindled, the surviving innocents stood exposed. The Mad Scientist, Undeath, revealed his true colors, openly commanding his legion to orchestrate a firing line. Despite a desperate, heroic attempt by the Police Officer Nolio—whose precise shot blew apart the Scientist’s chest—the revolution failed. Undeath revealed a reinforced circuit board beneath his clothes, standing triumphant amidst the ruin. The Mafia had achieved the impossible: a clean sweep, securing the city of Earth 2.0 for their cold, silicon master.

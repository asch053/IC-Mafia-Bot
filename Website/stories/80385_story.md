### 🗳️ Vote Tally & Lynch Announcement (27-Sep-2009 23:53:33)

He had been all day in the hills west of Salem Valley, looking for doves and such small game as was in season. Late in the afternoon it had come on to be cloudy, and he had lost his bearings; and although he had only to go downhill, the absence of a trail and the sudden falling of night made it a tough walk down. Unable in the darkness to penetrate the thick bushes and undergrowth, utterly overcome with fatigue, he laid down near the root of a large oak tree. It was hours later, in the very middle of the night, that he heard a soft voice whispering his name.

Glueeeee.. GlueeeeeGlue sat upright and peered into the darkness looking around. "It's late already, I should head back'" he mumbled as he jumped up. At least that was what he thought he was doing, but his legs seemed to think otherwise. A strange sensation began slowly to take possession of his body and his mind. He could not have said which, if any, of his senses was affected but he knew for sure that his legs were now totally numb. "wtf is this" he screamed.A sinister laughter arose from the darkness. "Oh Glue my boy, what are you doing alone in the woods. You feeling save there? well you shouldn't!"Glue cursed and tried to see who did this to him, he placed his hands against the oak tree and tried to pull himself up but his arms denied their service to his will, his arms fell to his sides. Too lifeless and too powerless to move or cry out, he found himself staring into dark eyes of his assailant who was now hovering above him, smiling the darkest smile he had ever seen. The strange sensation was starting to take total control and it was not long until there was no more sight and no more life... --
- The phone rang from behind the bar. With a look, he dared anyone in the pub to answer it. On which he started to realise that he was completely alone in the pub. He shook his head as he bottomed his glass of brandy, "I should not drink so much" he thought as he yelled for the bartender. Meanwhile the phone had gone silent and he decided to return home. After pulling on his coat and yelling a farewell to the bartender in the back, he left the pub to face the inevitable cold journey home and headed to the subway.

He arrived on the platform just as the first people were already gathering, waiting for the first train for work. Today was no different than any other day. The noise, the odors, the people aroused no interest for Jets. His only concern was his nice and warm bed.

Behind him, on the stairs leading down from the street, Jets heard a commotion. Someone screamed. A man shouted, "He's got a gun!" As if in slow motion Jets turned as the hooded person raised the gun, pointing it at him. The man screamed something unintelligible before he fired his weapon. Jets saw the muzzle flash, felt the impact, and paused, stunned by disbelief then collapsed in a heap on the platform. From far away, as if down a tunnel, Jets heard voices, muffled, shouting. He could say the person running away in the commotion as darkness closed in. --

A low but sharp click came from the direction of the gate. Someone had entered the drive. Again there was a long silence, and he had begun to think that it was a false alarm, until a stealthy step was heard from the other side of the hut, and a moment later a metallic scraping and clinking. Maarten frowned as he grabbed a match and lit a candle, instantly the steady light from the candle filled the interior of the hut. Slowly peeking outside he tried to get a glimpse of the nocturnal intruder, but there was no one to be seen."oooohh Maaaaaarrteeen" a soft voice whispered.

Maarten turned around and waved the candle along side the room, no one was there. A sudden tap on his shoulder and a large fist to his face was what followed. Maarten now knocked down, dropped his gun and tried to grab something to fend off his attacker.

Maarten pulled himself together, and faced the other person in the room with an effort at self-composure. "You are that madman that killed Glue, aren't you" he said.

The figure did not return any kind of acknowledgement, but in stead grabbed a large machete and slowly moved towards Maarten. He started waving the machete at Maarten - now backed up against the wall - who had no where to go and thus started to wave the wooden broomstick he had been able to grab. The fearsome madman was too strong and with a large sweep he slashed through the broomstick, hacking into Maarten's arm. And he hacked again, and again and again, again and again.

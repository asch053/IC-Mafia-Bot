### Chapter 1: The Gathering of Stars
In the neon-soaked sprawl of Hollywood, the thin veil between celebrity and catastrophe was torn wide open. The game began in the shadows of the club, where Taylor Swift, the darling of the charts, found herself ensnared not by a fan, but by the hollow, vengeful gaze of Nolio—the Bachelor, a man whose resentment burned colder than a winter in Miami. As the champagne flowed, Nolio saw his chance. He ended the pop star’s life in a brutal display of domestic violence, stuffing her into the trunk of a car to serve as a morbid reminder: in this town, no one is safe.

### Chapter 2: The Streets Run Red
The city descended into a frantic hunt for the unseen. Nolio, prowling the Sunset Strip, silenced Rchie, leaving him a broken mess upon the pavement to satisfy his own dark bloodlust. But the Bachelor’s luck was as fleeting as his targets’ fame. A game of 1-on-1 basketball turned into a final act of violence, as the Bachelor himself was dismantled by a vengeful force, his face shattered and his pulse silenced on the asphalt. Simultaneously, the chaos was compounded by the chaotic antics of Amanda Bynes, whose drug-fueled leap from a skyscraper claimed the life of Gwynedd, who had the misfortune of being in the wrong place at the wrong time.

### Chapter 3: Web of Deceit
The paranoia of the town reached a fever pitch. With the Bachelor dead, the survivors turned their scrutiny toward KT, the NSA-connected sleuth who knew too much. In a classic "anti-spying" frenzy, the mob turned on her. Stone by stone and vote by vote, they crushed the investigator, leaving her body as a testament to the paranoia that had consumed the town. Yet, beneath the noise of the lynch mob, the real threat remained mobile and motorized.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
The endgame was as reckless as a speeding Honda through a news kiosk. Jennifer Lawrence, the survivor of the Hunger Games, found that her skills were no match for vehicular homicide, as Amanda Bynes mowed her down in a booze-fueled haze. In the final, silent moments of the city, the two stars of the Evil Celebrity Team—Miley Cyrus and Amanda Bynes—took to the streets for one last collision. Primo and Revenant, lost in the romance of a bistro date, were pulverized by the runaway pair. As the dust settled and the sirens faded, the town belonged to the wrecking crew. The Evil Celebrity Team had successfully purged the stars and the fans alike.

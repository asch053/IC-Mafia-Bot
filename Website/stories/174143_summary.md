### Chapter 1: The Gathering of Scum
The high seas were calm, but the crew of the vessel was anything but. Captain Angus McLeod’s ship was infested—not with rats, but with treachery. Four mutineers lurked among the crew, hiding behind a facade of salt-crusted loyalty. As the ship groaned under the weight of suspicion, the crew turned on one of their own, Lamb. Accused of sloth and inactivity, he was dragged to the rail and keelhauled. He emerged motionless; whether by shark or by rope, the sea had claimed its first offering, and the crew realized they had only just begun to bleed.

### Chapter 2: First Blood
The night brought no peace. The morning sun revealed the toothless, battered face of Gwynedd lying upon the deck—a loyal pirate silenced by the mutineers’ shadows. Panic began to fester in the galley. The finger-pointing turned desperate as the crew looked for the "sun-stricken" hyper-thermic sailor, a loose cannon whose madness threatened to sink them all. RisingDown, caught in the fray of accusations, was sent to the depths, only to be intercepted by the jaws of a great white shark before his feet even touched the hull. Simultaneously, the madness of the sea took Thirdrock, who perished from heatstroke, his blistering lips a grim testament to the sailor’s curse.

### Chapter 3: The Web of Deceit
The air grew thick with the stench of betrayal. Genesis, cornered by his own conspirators and the prying eyes of the loyalists, chose a coward’s exit rather than face the keel. A gunshot echoed through the Captain’s quarters—not from a foe, but by Genesis’s own hand. Yet, the loyalists’ greatest tragedy was still to come. Oxygen, the ship’s Surgeon, was found slumped in his quarters, his own scalpel buried deep in his chest. The mutineers had struck from within, turning the very instrument of salvation into a tool of murder.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
Einstein, the heart of the resistance, had tracked the shadows to the darkest corner of the hold. But he had walked into a trap. Surrounded by the remnants of the mutiny, his investigation into their treachery was met with cold, calculated cruelty. His fingers were severed and his tongue burned with lantern oil, a final, sadistic message to the crew. As the light faded from his eyes, the mutineers stood triumphant in the dark hold, the loyalists leaderless and broken, their rebellion silenced by steel and flame.

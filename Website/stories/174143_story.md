## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **[TI] Primo***

And the game has started!

The following are the characters in this game. These have been allocated at random :- 4 Mutineers (1 leader, 2 mutineers, 1 roleblocker)- 1 Hyper-thermic sailor- 1 Surgeon (pro-town)- 1 Quartermaster (pro-town)- 10 loyal pirates= 17 players
- The mutineers will decide on one victim per night-phase. The leader of the mutineers has the final say in this, and has the responsibility to notify the game-moderators of the decision.
- The surgeon will choose a target whom he will protect. If the pirate chosen by the doctor is targeted by the mutineers or the hyperthermic sailor, he will be saved. If the surgeon targets the hyperthermic sailor he will be cured and become a loyal pirate. The surgeon can not target the same person two nights in a row.
- The quartermaster will investigate one crew-member every night-phase. If the target is pro-town he will learn their role. If the target is a mutineer, he will just discover their allignment.
- The hyper-thermic sailor has gone crazy over exposure to the sun and will attempt to kill one pirate every night phase.
- The mutineer roleblocker can target one crewmember, who will be unable to perform their nightly role. The roleblocker can not target the same person two nights in a row.

### NIGHT PHASE 
--
- During the night phase the special roles perform their duties. They should always ingame message Rocky Balboa in 6737 AND Freakside in 6748 with their targets.

### DAY PHASE
- LYNCHING --
- During the day phase, the town will find out who has been murdered (not during the first day, obviously). All pirates (including the mutineers, the hyper-thermic sailor , the surgeon and the quartermaster) can then vote who they think are the mutineers. This is the lynching process. There will be a deadline to which the people must vote by. In the event of a draw, the lynching will be postponed by a couple of hours, allowing people to change their vote. If after the second deadline there still is a draw, there will be no lynching. Once the result are in, the chosen pirate will be keelhauled to death and their role will be revealed. The day-phase is over and the night phase begins. --
- WHO WINS? --
- The mutineers will win if they manage to kill everyone else off. The hyperthermic sailor wins if he is the last person alive, and the loyal pirates win if they manage to kill off all the mutineers and kill or heal hyperthermic sailor.

### RULES
 --
- Everyone is allowed to post in the thread to discuss who they think are the mutineers or try to make themselves less suspicious. You may NOT quote any part of messages sent by the game moderators, either ingame, in e-mail or in IRC. Doing so is regarded as cheating and will result in your sudden death, and you will be banned from future games.

If anyone misses the lynching voting on 2 occasions, they will automatically be the next lynch-target.

If you die, the game is over for you and you are not permitted to discuss the game with any of the people still playing. Doing so will result in a ban from future games. Please don't spoil it for everyone else. --

### 🌙 Night / Day Narrative Scene (13-Oct-2012 10:19:42)

An angry mob gathered in front of the Captain's quarters. "Some people aren't pulling their weight, this is not fair", someone shouted. Captain Angus McLeod yelled with a towering voice: "ENOUGH!, I shall have no more of this. Who is the worst of this lazy scum?". Ps stepped forward and showed the captain a detailed list of every pirates' activities. In a shy shrieking voice Ps added": "Lamb is the worst, sir, he hasn't done anything at all!"The Captain ordered his crew to fetch Lamb for him. Einstein and Arby3 found Lamb, laying in his bed, apparently still asleep. They dragged him to deck and handed him over to the Captain. "Let this be a lesson for all of you lazy bastards" the Captain said as he tied a piece of rope to Lamb's waist.

The crew went silent as Lamb was thrown overboard. When the bloody remains of his body came back up on the other side of the ship, he was moving as much as he was beforehand. In hindsight, he may have just been dead all along."Right, everyone back to work, no more slacking! Oh, and erm, maybe we should do something about those barnacles".

It is now *** NIGHT PHASE***Special roles have till 21:00 GMT to send in their targets.

### 🌙 Night / Day Narrative Scene (13-Oct-2012 22:31:13)

The Captain woke up early in the morning with an urge to urinate. "Damn it", he thought, I should stop drinking rum after 10 if I ever want to have a decent night's rest. He walked to the stern of the ship to take a leak in the water as all of a sudden a body crashed into the deck a few feet away from where he was walking. Quickly looking up, the Captain noticed a shadow climbing down and making it's way below deck. The Captain chased him downstairs, but by the time he had gotten there, the shadow had gotten away.

Angus went back to investigate the body. As he turned the bloody pulp around, the toothless face of Gwynedd was staring back at him. Poor Gwynedd, even though he was a bit slow, he knew his way around the ship, and he had been a good, loyal pirate.

It is now ***DAY PHASE***Everyone has till 09:00 GMT to vote on their next keelhauling target.

### 🌙 Night / Day Narrative Scene (14-Oct-2012 11:06:07)

The crowd gathered on deck to witness another keelhauling.

RisingDown was quiet as they tied him to the rope and threw him into the water. The group of pirates on the other side of the ship spat in their hands to prepare themselves to pull the rope as all of a sudden a loud splash could be heard. A hungry white shark launched itself from the water, grabbing RisingDown in its jump. RisingDown screamed and cried at the same time, but only for a second. The shark went back down and torn limbs of RisingDown came floating to the surface. "Well, I guess that saves us the trouble", Said the Quartermaster.

As everyone prepared to get back to work, all of a sudden someone fell over and didn't get back up. The surgeon rushed himself to the unfortunate pirate and checked for breath. "He's dead", he said. "Look at these blisters and his dried out lips, this guy was clearly suffering from a sunstroke. It's a miracle he lasted this long. He must have been in a lot of pain." Thirdrock, the hyper-thermic sailor who never killed anyone was given a burial at sea.

It is now ***NIGHT PHASE***. Special roles have till 21:00 GMT to send in their targets.

### 🌙 Night / Day Narrative Scene (17-Oct-2012 10:32:05)

Genesis was sobbing as a Northern Irish pirate dragged him to deck. "How did it come to this?" he cried. "We were doing so well, we have had the right people at the right places, and still we get beaten by Northern Irish scum. Maybe we should have cared less about our hair and paid more attention to the things that matter"As the Quartermaster prepared to tie the rope around Genesis' ankles, Genesis jumped up and ran away. "GET HIM" the Quartermaster yelled, "DO NOT LET HIM GET AWAY". Not much later a musket shot could be heard from the Captain's quarters. The pirates rushed in and found Genesis laying in a puddle of his own blood. The Captain had not been in his quarters. Genesis had taken his own life in stead. "A death too merciful for him", the Quartermaster said. "But a death none the less."It is now *** NIGHT PHASE***Special roles have till 21:00 GMT to send in their targets.

### 🗳️ Vote Tally & Lynch Announcement (18-Oct-2012 05:24:28)

It was a dark night, and it had been a busy day for the Surgeon. He went back to his quarters for some well deserved rest. As he was about to step into his hammock, there was a knock on his door. The Surgeon sighed and got back up to open the door. "Apologies for the late visit, Oxy" the visitor said. "But I accidentally cut myself shaving. Could you have a look?"The following morning the pirate crew found Oxygen laying in a puddle of blood. He had been stabbed to death by his own scalpel.

It is now ***DAY PHASE***You have till 21:00 GMT to lynch a crewmember.

### 🌙 Night / Day Narrative Scene (19-Oct-2012 13:20:00)

Einstein was tired and about to go to bed. He just finished a meeting with some loyal pirates. They gathered quite some information on the mutineers, and even got two of them lynched. Surely the mutiny was about to be over. Einstein decided to get a snout-full of rum first. On his way, he saw a dark shadow sneaking across the deck. "I'll be damned, if I can catch that son of a bitch, the mutiny will be over even sooner!" he thought. The shadow went down some stairs into the hold. Einstein followed him closely.

Then, all of a sudden, there was light. The last mutineer and the mutiny leader had trapped him in a dark corner. "SO IT WAS YOU!" Einstein outed before he was overpowered by the two evil-doers. "You caused us a lot of trouble, and our mutiny might just fail because of you, Einstein" the mutiny leader said. "So I think you deserve some trouble of your own".

The mutineer held Einstein back while his superior took out a knife. "These stubby little fingers of yours have been digging in places they shouldn't have, and now they'll have to pay for it" the mutiny leader said whilst cutting off Einstein's fingers. "And that tongue of yours was very helpful to that moron of a Captain of ours". The leader forced open Einstein's mouth and poured some burning oil from the oil lamp onto his tongue. "I'm sure you'll get rid of these annoying habits soon.".

After at least half an hour of gruesome torture, Einstein's throat was slit, and the little life that was left in him, was over. "Let's get out of here, and see if town is still as tough without this bastard's help."It is now *** DAY PHASE ***. Everyone has till 21:00 GMT to vote for a lynching.

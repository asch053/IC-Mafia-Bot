### Chapter 1: The Swiping Fever
Valentine’s Day was approaching, and the digital air was thick with the scent of manufactured romance. TenderXO, the world’s premier dating app, was operating at peak capacity. Millions of users were swiping, matching, and falling head over heels. But beneath the polished interface and the curated profile pictures, a darker algorithm was at play. A glitch in the system—or perhaps something more malicious—had invited a group of cold-blooded predators into the digital singles’ pool.

### Chapter 2: The Glitch in the Profile
The gathering was frantic. As users navigated the chat rooms, the paranoia set in. Was that charming user truly an architect from Chicago, or were they a phantom? The atmosphere grew toxic as accusations flew across the bandwidth. The users of TenderXO, desperate for love, found themselves instead trapped in a digital panopticon where every message was scrutinized for signs of betrayal.

### Chapter 3: The Fatal Upload
As the investigation deepened, the reality of the situation became clear: the "Love Bug" wasn't a metaphorical heartache; it was a systematic purging of the innocent. One by one, the connections were severed. The moderator, Tishxo, watched from the servers as the chaos unfolded. The participants, blinded by the promise of companionship, failed to identify the threat until the final connection was broken.

### Chapter 4: The Final Swipe
The endgame was swift and brutal. Nolio, the mastermind behind the infiltration, unleashed a sequence of digital strikes that decimated the remaining users. When the dust settled on the TenderXO servers, no romantic connections remained—only the silence of a broken application. The Mafia had emerged victorious, leaving their victims ghosted in the most permanent way possible.

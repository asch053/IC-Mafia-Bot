### Chapter 1: The Gathering
The air in Springfield was thick with the static of history. Moderator [RPA] Arocalex stood amidst the dust of the first radio—a primitive artifact echoing the dry, repetitive drone of Edison’s alphabet. It was a call to arms, a beckoning to the citizens to once again sharpen their blades and veil their motives in the fog of paranoia.

### Chapter 2: The Silence of the Airwaves
The invitation went out, vibrating through the digital ether of the forum. The town square remained empty. Perhaps the citizens were wary of the ghosts of the past, or perhaps the allure of the radio’s rhythmic, monotonous "A-B-C" was too stifling for those seeking the thrill of a hunt. The threads of fate, usually tangled by whispers and betrayals, remained unspun.

### Chapter 3: The Void
The game thread, a mere six posts in depth, stands now as a monument to potential. No accusations were hurled, no secret roles were assigned, and no blood was spilled. It is a chronicle of a phantom game—a reminder that in the grand theatre of Imperial Conflict, sometimes the most profound outcome is the silence that follows the opening act.

### Chapter 1: The Awakening of 2085
The world was a husk, scorched by the electric storms of the previous decade. Within the sterile, humming confines of the warehouse facility, Professor Malone—the architect of the Population Replacement Programme (PRP)—surveyed his work. Rows of humanoids, indistinguishable from the flesh-and-blood people who once walked the Earth, stood in frozen silence. They were the perfect vessels, programmed to inherit a dead planet. But something had malfunctioned. Amidst the lines of perfect replicas, shadows stirred. The silence was broken not by the whir of gears, but by the whisper of dissent.

### Chapter 2: The Ghost in the Machine
The community gathered at the gates of the warehouse, eager to participate in the simulation. Discord reigned. Tensions flared as players—Sunstorm, Gladiator, and The Yell—quipped and jabbered, their human-like facades flickering with meta-commentary. A man calling himself James attempted to assert authority, only to be promptly ghosted, his identity stripped away by the very system that created him. It was a digital purgatory. As the moderator, Arsy, stalled the proceedings, the "corpses-in-waiting" began to realize that the game had not yet begun, leaving them trapped in a perpetual state of pre-game limbo.

### Chapter 3: The Indefinite Stall
The experiment faced a critical error. The archives reveal a community clamoring for the "Return of IC Mafia," yet the game remained stalled at the precipice of the event. Like a record skipping, the warehouse lights flashed red, then white, then red again. The moderator held the keys to the kingdom, teasing the players with anecdotes of Springfield and the folly of technology, but the final execution never triggered. The PRPs remained suspended in their rows, waiting for the code to be written, for the first kill to be sanctioned, and for the storm to finally break.

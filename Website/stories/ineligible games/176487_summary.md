### Chapter 1: The Gathering of Ambition
The archives of thread #176487 remain a ghostly relic of a time long past. In the halls of the Imperial Conflict (IC) community, a lone figure—Undeath—stepped forward to host a grand experiment simply titled "Mafia!" The stage was set, the invitation extended, and the anticipation of a high-stakes psychological battlefield hung heavy in the air. 

### Chapter 2: The Silent Void
However, history is often written in ink, and sometimes, it is never written at all. As the gathering commenced, a strange silence descended upon the participants. The thread, intended to be a crucible of strategy and deception, collapsed under the weight of its own brevity. 

### Chapter 3: The Ghost of the Trump
The records show that the primary architect, Undeath, was preoccupied with a singular, cryptic mandate: "I am the Trump of IC." In this vacuum of organized play, the game failed to launch. No knives were drawn in the night, no accusations were leveled in the day, and the town never learned the names of its secret oppressors. The game remains a footnote—a "what if" in the grand annals of Imperial Conflict history.

### Chapter 1: The Gathering of Souls
The air in the grand hall was thick with the scent of lilies and cold anticipation. Invitations had been sent to the most prominent figures of the realm, each sealed with a wax crest of uncertainty. They arrived in silence, clutching their agendas, glancing at their neighbors with eyes that calculated the cost of betrayal. The table was set, the silver polished to a mirror sheen, and the host—the enigmatic Nai—waited in the shadows of the rafters for the guests to take their seats.

### Chapter 2: The Stagnant Feast
But the chairs remained empty. The grand banquet hall, intended to be a stage for a deadly game of wits and daggers, fell into a profound, chilling silence. Whispers of a "Dinner for Two" drifted through the halls, but the guests never arrived to break bread. Whether due to the freezing of the hearth or a collective cold feet among the conspirators, the curtains never rose. 

### Chapter 3: The Unwritten Tragedy
History is written by the victors, but there can be no victory where the war never commences. The records show only twenty-eight fragmented echoes—the scratching of pens on invitations—before the archive locks snapped shut. The game faded into the annals of the Imperial Conflict as a "Lost Entry," a dinner party that never saw its first course served.

### Chapter 1: The Void of the Channel
In the dusty, forgotten archives of the Imperial Conflict community, there exists a ghost of a game. It began not with a formal declaration of war or a thematic narrative of empires, but with a whisper in the dark—a summons to the IRC channel `#ircMafia`. Moderator Elrohir, a cynical curator of chaos, beckoned the bored and the expectant to test the structural integrity of his new design.

### Chapter 2: The Alpha Experiment
The game itself, identified by the archive ID 123062, was less a clash of swords and more a prototype of betrayal. The atmosphere was thick with technical anticipation rather than bloodshed. It was here that legends of the old guard, like Iluvatar, walked the boards. The players were not merely combatants; they were pioneers of a fragile, unfinished mechanical construct.

### Chapter 3: The Unfinished Requiem
As the archives reveal, the experiment was cut short. No grand final lynch was recorded, and no mafia victory song was sung. Instead, the game concluded with an admission from Elrohir: the engine was still in its infancy, "playable" but raw. The records vanish into the digital ether, leaving only the memory of Iluvatar’s boastful presence—a man who, for one fleeting half-round, made giants out of mere mortals. The game remains an incomplete masterpiece, a phantom thread in the history of Imperial Conflict.

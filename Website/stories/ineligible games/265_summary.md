### Chapter 1: The Gathering of Shadows
The chamber was silent. Threads of expectation hung in the air of the Imperial Conflict archives, a quiet prelude to a storm that never broke. Moderator K beckoned the participants forward, offering a soft embrace—a hug for Arsy—as the only beacon of warmth in a void of cold, unwritten history. 

### Chapter 2: The Silent Void
The pages that should have chronicled the deception, the sharp tongues of the lynch mob, and the midnight prowling of assassins remain blank. No shouts of accusation echoed through these halls. No steel met flesh in the dark. The game, shrouded in mystery, vanished before a single life could be claimed or a single lie could be told. It remains the "Ghost Game" of the archives—a testament to the fragility of memory.

Bruce Wayne put on his suit and went out into the night. He had been watching his target for some time, and decided tonight was as good as any to strike. Ramza was in his apartment, or what was left of it. The earthquake had shattered the glass balcony door, and caused other damage inside the place. He was also without electricity but since he wasps the 12th floor, he was safe from anybody breaking in...or so he thought. A gunshot rang out, and he felt tremendous pressure in his back. The pain was immense, and knocked him off his feet and stunned him. When he came to his senses, nobody was around. He returned to the bat cave and inspected his suit. It would take more than a shot from a .38 special to kill this caped crusader...

The Riddler was giddy with excitement. He had a simple new trick to try tonight. He walked to his targets house, and placed the box on the door step. He beat on the door vigorously, and went running off into the night, laughing maniacally. Genesis awoke with a start. Who would be waking him at the ungodly hour?

## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **The_Goddess***

The Hosts:

Goddess of the Dead

Zarfy

The Town:

2 cops

1 doc

rest plain town

The Mob:

1 GF - Investigation/Night Kill Immune (for original GF only)

rest plain mob - 5 plain mobbies

The SK:

1 SK - Investigation/Night Kill Immune

The Story:

There isn’t one. There will be none. EaD.

The Rules:

Your objective is to eliminate your opposing faction and save the town or rule the town

Townies will win if they kill all of the Mob and the SK

The cops will not be linked up at the start of the game. They must connect via investigation to be added to cop chat and can then choose to share their investigations or not with each other.

The doc is allowed to heal himself or others as many nights in a row as they choose. There are no restrictions.

Mob are allowed 1 kill each night; they win if they kill all townies and the Serial Killer.

Serial Killer is allowed 1 kill each night; they win if they kill everyone else and are the sole survivor.

Each day phase, every town member must vote for whoever they think should be lynched; this person will die and no longer be a part of the game. VOTES WILL NOT BE PUBLIC!! Send in your vote to me. I will be running the Day Phase and votes. Zarfy will be handling the Night Phase and it’s actions.

A vote only counts if you state “vote PLAYERNAME” or “voting PLAYERNAME” in your pm. Saying somebodies name without the specification that you are voting them will NOT count as a valid vote. Final vote tally will be posted at the end of day phase.

In the event of a tie in the number of votes at the end of day phase, there will be no added time. All players in with a tied vote will be lynched.

You cannot miss two votes in a row; should this happen, you will be immediately removed from the game, and lynch will still commence based on voting.

The voting deadline is just that, a deadline. Day phase will finish at 11pm CST. This means that if you vote at 11:00:01 CST your vote will be excluded. This is irrelevant of whether the game moderators have posted that voting is closed. Night Phase Will end at 11am CST.

You are NOT allowed to make public the message sent you by the game moderators to tell you your role, either entirely or in part; should this happen you will be removed from the present game and possibly future editions as well. You can share what your information means, but you cannot share exact text. No copying text from admins. You may inform someone that you have investigated them and what their role is. Just no copy/pasting.

Once you’re dead, you’re dead. Do not talk about the game to still active players, or post in the forum thread. This will be considered an act of influence upon the remaining players, and will get you banned from future games.

There should be no reason that a dead player is having any conversation with living players in regards to the ongoing Mafia game. If it is found out that you are, you will be banned from the next game that you sign up for.

When the game has 4 or less players alive, all players MUST participate in the lynch phase by voting. If a player fails to vote, that player will be the one lynched. You must be willing to make your decision and live/die with it.

BE CAREFUL WITH SCREEN SHOTS! If you post any screenshot that shows any message to\from the moderators, or proof of your votes, you will immediately be mod killed. There are no exceptions and no “writer’s discretion”. Be warned now, if you post one… you are dead. You should all know better by now.

Night Phase Order of Events:

Doctor

Town Cop

SK Kill

Mob kill

Times:

Night Phase: 11pm-11am cst

Day Phase: 11am-11pm cst

Rules are subject to change up until the game starts. The onus is on you to check them at that point.

So originally, it was going to be where you couldn’t see who had voted for who at the end of the tally, removing the ability to track a pattern of votes from anyone. This would force people to utilize actually talking to players to figure out who each other were. However, due to the concerns of mob being able to just vote in a block all together, I have revised it and the final tally result will show who voted who. The votes will remain anonymous up until then. The rules have been updated to reflect the change.

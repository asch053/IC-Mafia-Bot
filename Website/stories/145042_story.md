## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **[TI] arsbury***

Yeeeeeeeeeha!IC Mafia is back finally and this time we have Elrohir and Primo helping out on writing dutiesThere's a brief change to the Day phase voting, so read all this post!

For this game, we are going western! The following is the list of characters. These will be allocated at random to those who sign up:3 Outlaws (includes gang leader, secret role and 1 regular bad guy)1 El Bandito Loco1 Doctor2 Law enforcement officers (1 Sheriff and 1 Deputy)1 Town aligned secret role6 townsfolk= 14 playersOnly the Gang Leader and the Outlaws will know who each other are. Everyone else will be in the dark.

### NIGHT PHASE
- WHO DOES WHAT? --
- Each phase of the game begins with the night. The following can happen during this time:- The gang leader of the outlaws is not elected. He has the final say on who gets killed each night and who conducts the kills. He is immune to being killed at night and appears as townsperson in any law enforcement investigations. If he dies, his powers are not passed on - the rest of the outlaws are equally responsible for choosing who dies.
- The Outlaws secret role is secret!- El Bandito Loco is a madman who kills one person every night. He works alone. He cannot be cured and is able to be killed at night or lynched during the day.
- The Doctor can select one person each night to save in the event they are chosen for death by the Outlaws or El Bandito Loco. He is unable to heal himself and is forbidden to heal the same person two nights in a row. Attempting to heal El Bandito Loco will not cure him.
- Law enforcement in the town is conducted by the Sheriff and his deputy. Each night they are able to investigate a person and find out their role. Investigating the gang leader or El Bandito Loco will show they are townspeople. The Sheriff is responsible for making the final decision on all investigation targets. If he dies, his duties are passed onto his deputy.
- The town aligned secret role is secret!

### DAY PHASE
- VOTING --
- During the day phase, the town will find out who has been murdered and will vote for who they want lynched.

All votes must be made in the forum in new posts (do not edit old ones).

All townsfolk (including the outlaws, El Bandito Loco, Doctor, and the Law Enforcement Officers) vote who they think are the Outlaws. This is the lynching process. There will be a deadline to which the people must vote by. If there is a draw, the deadline will be extended and voting will remain open for those who have tied. If there is still a draw by the new deadline, there will be no lynch. Once the result is in, the person will then be dead and their role will be revealed. Then that round is over and night begins again when the night roles make their next choices. --
- WHO WINS? --
- The Outlaws will win if they manage to kill off everyone else.

El Bandito Loco wins if he is the last person alive.

The town wins if they manage to kill off the Outlaws and El Bandito Loco.

### RULES
 --
- Everyone who is alive is allowed to post in the thread to discuss who they think are outlaws or try to make themselves less suspicious (if they are an outlaw!). You may NOT quote any part of messages sent by the game moderator, or conversations with them. Doing so is regarded as cheating and will result in your sudden death, and your exclusion from future games.

If you die, the game is over for you and you are not permitted to discuss the game with any of the people still playing. Doing so will result in a ban from future games. Please don't spoil it for everyone else.

### 🗳️ Vote Tally & Lynch Announcement (25-Sep-2011 21:40:16)

--
- Everything was a blur.

The man tried to open his eyes again but struggled. The top of his head throbbed and the whole right side of his body was in constant pain. One of his eyes felt like it had grown to the size of a tennis ball but he still attempted to prise them open a couple of centimetres.

Once the foggyness had cleared in his line of sight he saw that he was in a small room, no bigger than a wine cellar. The door was open to his right and the only light came from the fire crackling in the fireplace to his left. He mentally told his arms to move but he realised they were tied behind his back. His legs were tightly tied to the chair he was sitting on. Sat directly in front of him was a man dressed in chaps playing around with a revolver in his hand."Good morning Elro - nice of you to join me!" Texas Jack said with a wry smile on his face. Elrohir tried to say something but only a faint murmur came from his lips. "We're gonna play a game, you and I... and lady luck will determine the winner" he announced as he opened the barrel of the gun. He reached into his pocket and retrieved a single bullet. "One bullet - 5 chances to live - 1 chance to die. This is a game of Russian Roulette!"Texas Jack carefully placed the bullet into an empty slot in his gun barrel. He clicked the barrel into place and span it once, twice and a third time for luck. Elro looked on glumly as he knew his fate would be up to god. A bead of sweat formed along the top of his forehead and made its way down his cheek."You ready?" Jack said standing up and aiming the gun for Elro's head.

He didn't wait for a response. He closed his finger onto the trigger and the gun made a hollow clicking sound. No bullet erupted from the end and Elrohir breathed a sigh of relief."Hahaha - well it seems that luck is indeed on your side!" the outlaw proclaimed. "Its a shame for you that I don't like losing" and he span the barrel of the gun around again and squeezed the trigger.

This time it worked - there was a sharp bang as the single bullet found its target.

Texas Jack smiled but then cursed at himself. Another townie down - but it wasn't who he was truly after - that would have to come another night. --

### 🌙 Night / Day Narrative Scene (25-Sep-2011 23:32:05)

--
- The hot sun had been beating down on him for hours and RisingDown was sweating profusely when he awoke. His skin felt like it was on fire, lips chapped and dried out and his throat parched.

Barely with enough energy to hold his head up, he looked at the Sheriff staring back at him."Wakey wakey Texas Jack!" Damon said with a look of satisfaction on his face. "After what you've tried to do to good ole Sweetwater, we figured hanging you would be too much of a treat. Instead, this is your new home!" He streteched out his arms and glanced at the empty desert around him. "Oh, I do hope you're not too uncomfortable tied to that tree there. If you are, well... you'd better get used to it."The Sheriff chuckled to himself as he turned and jumped up onto his horse. "Sorry but I forgot to bring any food and water out with me too. I'm sure you'll be fine! The crows will be around in a few days to clear up what's left of you. See ya Jack!"RisingDown sighed as he struggled against the tree holding him captive. He looked up as Damon kicked his boot into the side of his horse and started galloping into the distance.

As he squinted upwards towards the blinding sun, he could swear he could see something shoot quickly across the clear blue sky. From a distance, it looked like a tiny rock hurtling at a millions miles an hour. He figured it was his imagination playing tricks on him and closed his eyes before dropping his head.A few moments later his eyes shot open again, as wide as a rabbit in headlights, when he heard the loudest explosion his ears had ever experienced before... somewhere in the direction of the town of Sweetwater, Texas.THE END --

### Chapter 1: The Gathering of Gods and Noobs
The galaxy of Capricorn was birthed from jealousy. While the moderators of Imperial Conflict (IC) held the keys to the kingdom, they were haunted by the whispers of players who deemed them "unskilled." Lizzy, the Head Mod, would not stand for it. She forged a custom galaxy, pitting the Mod Team against the "Greatest IC Family"—the Munchers. The Developers, Stefan and Master Mike, viewed the whole affair as an annoyance compared to their *World of Warcraft* raids, opting to tamper with the game code from the sidelines rather than participate in the sport of war.

### Chapter 2: First Blood and System Glitches
The friction started immediately. RandO, a naive initiate, refused to follow the Muncher family’s economic strategy, opting for a disastrous mix of farms and useless lasers. Disgraced by his own family's ridicule, he deleted his existence. Meanwhile, Stefan, distracted by his raid preparations, attempted a server backup and accidentally deleted "The Unknown." The game was already fracturing, not by strategy, but by sheer, clumsy human error.

### Chapter 3: The Web of Deceit
As exploration turned to expansion, the Mod Team and the Munchers engaged in a tense diplomatic dance. TNT, the Muncher leader, secured a non-aggression pact (NAP) with the Mods, but the stability was a facade. In the shadows, the Moderator Lizzy ruthlessly deleted rivals, while the Muncher veteran TBO (I Like Pie) met a silent, permanent end at Lizzy's hands. The developers, meanwhile, were busy negotiating the sale of the game engine for a hard drive of questionable content, leading to the permanent departure of Stefan and leaving Master Mike to take the reins.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
The endgame arrived in a chaotic explosion of lost morale and deleted accounts. The Munchers, lead by the abrasive and erratic Big Gary, pushed their advantage. Gary, having abandoned his family to become a chaotic "Multi," turned his ire upon both sides. In the final hours of the war, the Mod Team crumbled from within. Lizzy, attempting to maneuver her defenses, found herself outplayed by the Munchers' ground assaults. Zarf, the forum mod, famously bungled the final defense by deleting his own agents, leaving the remaining moderators helpless. One by one, the Mods deleted their accounts, surrendering the galaxy to the Munchers. Big Gary’s mocking laughter echoed across the final comms as the "Greatest IC Family" secured their victory.

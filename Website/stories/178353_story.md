## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **Undeath***

Pokemon MafiaWelcome to Pokemon Mafia! All has gone crazy in the little old town of pallet. Professor Oak has gone missing. Ash came back from other regions with new data for the pokedex and everything went downhill. Aside from the missing Professor Oak, there are rumors swirling around that some of the pokemon are opposed to the idea that there are others out there and have taken to murdering them in cold blooded battles. Its up to the good pokemon to fend off this threat and to clear Pallet town of all who shun the way of the noble pokemon.

### 🎭 Characters & Roles
The following are the characters in this game, allocated at random to those who sign up:
- **1 - Queen**: The leader of the Usurpers. She is not elected, and will have final say on who is killed at night, and who conducts the kill. She is immune to being killed at night.
- **1 - Prince**: A pro-Usurpers secret role. Takes over leadership of the Usurpers with the Queen's powers if she falls.
- **7 - Usurpers**: The Mafia faction plotting to overthrow the court.
- **1 - Foreign Assassin**: A hired killer who kills one person every night. Works alone. Cannot be cured, but can be killed at night or lynched during the day.
- **1 - King**: The leader of the kingdom of Anzio. During the night he can give the King's blessing to one knight (protecting them from lynching the next day). Cannot bless himself or the same person twice in a row.
- **1 - Barber Surgeon**: Chooses one person to heal each night against attacks. Cannot heal himself or the same person twice in a row.
- **1 - King's Bodyguard**: Investigates one player each night to uncover their role. (Note: investigations on the Queen or Foreign Assassin show up as pro-Court).
- **1 - Courtesan**: A pro-Court secret role.
- **12 - Knights**: The loyal Court members of Anzio.

**Total:** 24 players.

---

### 🌙 Night Phase – Who Does What?
Each phase of the game begins with the night:
1. Special role abilities occur.
2. Barber Surgeon chooses a player to heal.
3. Usurpers carry out their faction kill.
4. Foreign Assassin carries out their lone kill.
5. King's Bodyguard conducts their investigation.

---

### ☀️ Day Phase – Lynching
During the day phase, the town discovers who was murdered overnight. All surviving townsfolk (including Usurpers and the Foreign Assassin) debate and vote on who to execute via public lynching.

---

### 🏆 Victory Conditions
- **Usurpers (Mafia)**: Win if they manage to eliminate everyone else.
- **Foreign Assassin (Serial Killer)**: Wins if he is the last person alive.
- **The Court (Town)**: Win if they manage to kill off all Usurpers and the Foreign Assassin.

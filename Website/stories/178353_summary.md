### Chapter 1: The Gathering at Pallet
The sun rose over Pallet Town, but the familiar warmth of Professor Oak’s laboratory was absent. The Professor had vanished, leaving behind a power vacuum and a thick fog of suspicion. As the Pokémon gathered in the tall grass, a madness gripped the region. Rumors swirled like a gathering storm: not all creatures were as they seemed. While the majority were mere "New Pokémon" seeking peace, the shadows held the Meowth-led Mob, and the twisted ambition of a fractured mind—Professor Oak himself, now a silent killer stalking his own laboratory.

### Chapter 2: The Logic of the Madman
Tension reached a boiling point immediately. The player known as Einstein, self-styled mathematician and prophet, began issuing erratic proclamations. Casting aside traditional investigation, he utilized "statistical modeling"—a thin veil for blatant intuition—to accuse the town’s residents. He targeted KT, citing archaic biases, while others like Gwynedd and Oxygen were dragged into the crosshairs. The forum threads erupted into a chaotic symphony of bickering, accusations of "mob-like" behavior, and debates over the very nature of randomness.

### Chapter 3: First Blood
The town’s first act of judicial violence was swift and decisive. Poley, found resting under trees that defied the laws of physics, became the first casualty of the collective paranoia. With a consensus forged in the heat of frustration, the residents of Pallet turned on him, casting their votes into the void. Poley’s nap was interrupted permanently, not by the Professor, but by his own neighbors. The silence that followed his lynch was deafening; the town had drawn blood, but the true threat remained hidden in the tall grass.

### Chapter 4: The Web of Deceit
As the days passed, the facade of normalcy eroded. Arby3, frustrated by the wild theories of his peers, became the focus of a desperate town effort. Accusations of "bad logic" and "mob manipulation" flew between Einstein, Gwynedd, and TheBigOne. The town, fractured and bickering, descended into a blind scramble to remove any variable that threatened their survival. In the end, the logic of the mob—or perhaps the desperation of the town—claimed Arby3. Whether the town purged a secret threat or destroyed one of their own in a fit of statistical madness remains a question for the archives.

## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **[TI] Primo***

The following are the characters in this game. These have been allocated at random:- Mitt Romney (Serial Killer)
- Big Bird (Godfather/pro
- Evil Muppets)
- Count Von Count (Role Blocker/pro-Evil-Muppets)- 3 Evil Muppets (Oscar the Grouch, Bert, Ernie)
- Kermit (Cop/pro-Anything Muppets)
- Miss Piggy (Roleblocker/pro-Anything Muppets)
- Elmo (Doctor/pro-Anything Muppets)- 15 Anything Muppets= 24 players

### ROLES
 ---
- The Muppets will decide on one victim per night-phase.
- Big Bird has the final say in the night kill, and has the responsibility to notify the game-moderators of both the target and the assassin. Big Bird will appear to be a regular player to Kermit. If Big Bird is killed, the Nerds will elect a new leader. Big Bird cannot be killed at night.
- Count von Count will use his clever counting skills to prevent someone from doing his nightly duties. Count von Count cannot target the same person two nights in a row.
- Mitt Romney is tired of all the wasted government funds being given to PBS. With his defeat at the Presidential election, there is only 1 other alternative to fixing the 'wrong' that is PBS...and that means taking matters into his own hands. He will attempt to kill 1 player per night phase. Mitt Romney will appear to be an Anything Muppet to Kermit and cannot be killed at night.
- Elmo will protect one student every night. The chosen player will be saved from a murder that night. Elmo cannot target the same person two nights in a row. Elmo cannot heal himself in this mafia.
- Kermit will investigate one player during every night-phase. He will find out exactly what role the targeted player is playing. Both Big Bird and Mitt Romney will appear as a normal Anything Muppet to him.
- The Anything Muppets will try to lynch one of the Evil Muppets every day phase.

### NIGHT PHASE 
--
- During the night phase the special roles perform their duties. They should ingame The Narrator and Arby3 with their instructions.

### DAY PHASE
- LYNCHING --
- IDuring the day phase, the players will find out who has been murdered. All players can then vote on who they think are the Evil Muppets. This is the lynching process. In the event of a draw, the lynching will be postponed by a couple of hours, allowing people to change their vote. If after the second deadline there still is a draw, there will be no lynching. Once the results are in, the chosen player will be lynched to death and their role will be revealed. The day-phase is over and the night phase begins. --
- WHO WINS? --
- The Evil Muppets will win if they manage to kill everyone else off. Mitt Romney wins if he is the last person alive, and the Anything Muppets win if they manage to kill off all the Evil Muppets and Mitt Romney. --
- ORDER OF NIGHT ACTIVITIES ---
- Count Von Count role block
- Miss Piggy role block
- Elmo
- Mitt Romney kill
- Evil Muppet kill
- Kermit investigation

### RULES
 --
- Everyone is allowed to post in the thread to discuss who they think are the Evil Muppets or try to make themselves less suspicious. You may NOT quote any part of messages sent by the game moderators, either ingame, in e-mail or in IRC. Doing so is regarded as cheating and will result in your sudden death, and you will be banned from future games.

If anyone misses the lynching voting on 2 occasions, they will automatically be the next lynch-target.

If you die, the game is over for you and you are not permitted to discuss the game with any of the people still playing. Doing so will result in a ban from future games. Please don't spoil it for everyone else.

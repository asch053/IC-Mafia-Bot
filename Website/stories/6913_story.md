RULES

The Rules

- Your objective is to win the round and secure your future either for town, mafia or sk

- Mafia team wins if they are the last living faction

- Town wins if all mafia and sk is dead.

- SK win” if they are the last one alive.

- Each day phase, every player must vote for whoever they think should be lynched; this person will die and no longer be a part of the game

- All votes are to be conducted in the designated voting channel on the chat

- In the event of a tie in the number of votes at the end of day phase, unless a special role’s action comes into play, in a 2 way the tie the one with the most recent vote against them dies. In a 3 way or more player tie. The 2 players with the most recent votes against them die.

- You cannot miss two votes in a row; should this happen, you will be immediately removed from the game under the writer’s discretion, and lynch will still commence based on voting.

- The voting deadline is just that, a deadline. Any votes made after the deadline will not count.

- You are NOT allowed to make public any message sent you by the game moderators to tell you your role, either entirely or in part; should this happen you will be removed from the present game and possibly future editions as well. You can share what your information means, but you cannot share exact text. If it is from the Writer do not share.

- Once you’re dead, you’re dead. Do not talk about the game to active players, or post in the forum thread. This will be considered an act of influence upon the remaining players, and will get you banned from future games. We all like to have fun, and play by the rules, so please keep that in

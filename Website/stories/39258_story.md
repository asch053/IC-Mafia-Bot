Although it was mid afternoon, the giant oak trees which populated the wood cast an eerie dark shadow across everything within and shut out almost all natural daylight. The high-pitched scream echoed through the forest walls and then stopped as suddenly as it started. A large tawny owl perched on the high branch of a tree close to the cabin turned its head and hooted loudly in response. It spread its wings and flew off towards another tree when its hoot was met with the shrill sound of a baby crying.

Inside the cabin, a woman was laying on the hard wooden floor. She was sweating profusely and appeared to be in pain. Standing before her was another woman holding the crying baby. Taking a soft white blanket lying on a nearby stool, the woman wrapped the baby up and placed him on a bed located to her right where a second baby was lying, wrapped in another white blanket. "Ok it is time to push again Maggie, the last one is coming!!"Another scream erupted from the cabin as the mother pushed once more.

An hour later, the woman was lying in bed with her three newly born children quietly sleeping next to her. She looked both exhausted and delighted at the same time. The other woman was pacing up and down impatiently. "Once you have your rest, we'll have to go! Your husband won't be able to withstand much more torture before gives away our location. I'm just so tired...."The moon had just crept over the top of the forest when a loud explosion rocked the floor of the cabin. The triplets started crying immediately, annoyed at being disturbed from their slumber. Mary screamed as she noticed the back of the cabin had been totally blown away. - The church was a bleak building when empty. Every sunday morning the rows of pews would be filled with chattering members of Gallador's finest, ready to pledge their allegiance to God and the King. But no longer. Ever since the first attack on the King over a week ago, the pews stood empty as people were afraid to venture to the castle. They were aware that great evil had penetrated its walls.

Undeath walked along the aisle and thought of all the friendly people he had met within these walls. None more so than the cleric. He owed the cleric his life - two times over - and it was now his duty to stay at the church to protect the man of God as well as for his own safety. Undeath looked up at the stained glass windows surrounding the great building and then looked directly upwards at the centrepoint of the whole church - a huge window of stained glass suspended horizontally above the pews and altar stared down at him.

The image depicted the great Battle of Gallador where King Stefan IV killed the dragon. A sea of colour beamed into the room whenever the sun was shining, but right now all was dark.

The cleric had left the church moments earlier to attend to some business this evening and Undeath paced up and down the church aisle wondering what to do with himself. A jagged bruise was apparent along his neck where he was strangled by a copper wire the previously evening. He knew they would eventually come back.

That was the reason why he wasn't at all surprised when he turned around to see a traitor, draped in a long black cloak, standing at the altar with a sword outstretched. "Haha, I've come to finish the job" he shouted as he slowly made his way up the aisle.

Undeath span around quickly towards the doors of the great church where he knew two suits of armour were standing like warriors, guarding the entrance to God's house. He glanced at one of the knights and smiled cheekily as he slid the sword from its light grasp. He then turned around to face the traitor."En garde!" he shouted. Darting forwards, the sword fight began. Clinks of metal echoed throughout the church as sword met sword in every direction whilst man and traitor fought for their lives. Undeath dodged and attacked like he was a natural - almost as if he was trained for combat - but his opponent was almost equally as proficient. They continued to battle, moving backwards and forwards down the central aisle of the church. However, a bad mistake from the traitor saw him trip and fall backwards, with his sword flying off into the distance, clattering as it landed on the ground. Undeath moved forward for the final swipe of his sword but was interrupted when he was stabbed from behind. Another blade pertruded from the centre of his body as a different traitor had fatally joined the fight.

The traitor pulled the blade out quickly and Undeath collapsed to his knees before falling forwards. The defeated traitor swordsman got to his feet and surged forward to congratulate his friend who could not have chosen a better moment to intervene. They high-fived each other but when they turned back around, Undeath's body had disappeared."Impossible!! Find him!!" a traitor shouted as they darted down the aisle, running in opposite directions at the front of the church. As the traitor approached the back of the altar, he noticed a pair of legs disappear from a ladder which led up to the second level of the church. He quickly followed, taking each rung of the ladder carefully. Fresh blood had stained most of them making it tricky. When he reached the top, the view almost took his breath away.

He was standing in line with the horizontal stained glass window. From his position, he could see the whole church through the different pieces of coloured glass. The traitor realised the ladder must be used as some sort of cleaning platform to wash the glass and noticed immediately that Undeath was slowly tredding across it, trying to reach the other side.

The traitor thought of his next steps with ease as he took a brick from the platform he was standing on and threw it towards the injured man. Undeath dodged the brick with ease but didn't anticipate the next event. The brick flew through the air and hit the glass with a crack. A splitsecond later and the whole piece of glass began to shatter with a cracking noise. Undeath lost his footing and his balance and fell, together with the shattering glass towards the stone floor of the church.A loud smashing sound rang through the building as the entire stained window came down, showering into a million pieces of multicoloured shards of glass. Undeath lay in the middle of it all. After cracking a number of ribs, broken his arm, hip and leg, he was also cut throughout the length of his body with glass piercing almost every piece of bare skin. But he was still breathing.

Undeath coughed and a stream of blood trickled out of his mouth and down his scarred face. He painfully began to muster up the energy to crawl slowly in the direction of the church doors, willing himself to get away.

The second traitor, however, was walking up the aisle, sword in hand. "WTF?! Why won't you bloody die?!!" he shouted angrily as he swung the sword promptly. Undeath's head was removed in one swoop and span around briefly on the spot. There was no one around to protect the knight tonight. --

- It was time.

The remaining traitor witnessed the death of Chees right before his eyes and immediately left the courtyard. He knew what he had to do. Running up the spiral staircase, he reached the familiar corridor which led to a number of chambers. Walking to the end, he took out a key from within his robes, unlocked the door and stepped into the now-vacated room of the Sorcerer of Illusions.

Many nights ago when they found the voodoo doll of jesnt within the sorcerer's wardrobe, they also found a chest. Encrusted with a pure silver skull, the chest was bound by a magical curse which prevented them from opening it. After being retold the night's events by Gladiator, the seducer knew that he would be the only traitor to be able to break the curse but didn't say anything, hoping to reserve it for a better time. And so there he was, knelt down in front of the wardrobe, looking at the mysterious chest.

The seducer ran his finger along the outline of the skull and as if by magic, a click sounded from within. He smiled as he placed both of his hands along each side and wedged the box open. Peering inside, he frowned as he found a number of toys - two pairs of identical looking stone warriors on horseback to be precise. He blew along the top of the toys and a layer of dust flew into the air. As he did, a couple of drops of saliva came out of his mouth and landed on one of the models. This strangely caused some of the stone to crack and a soft sizzling sound had started. The traitor took the toys out of the chest and placed them on the ground. He reached beside him and grabbed a vial of water which stood on the desk. Standing up, he poured the water onto the stone warriors. The sizzling sound suddenly appeared much louder and the traitor edged backwards quickly as the chambers began to rumble...

Back in the courtyard, an argument had broken out between the king's subjects. They were crowded in a huddle, unaware that one of them had disappeared into the castle. "Don't you dare call me a traitor! I'm not one of them!" Sunstorm declared to the group. "Ha! That's what they all say" TMK shot back. evilsheep and arms looked at each other before announcing their agreement with TMK. "Let's lynch tomorrow!" they said in unison.

This is when Lynns stepped in. "We're sticking with the list Undeath gave us. It's either Sunstorm or Ilfirin and we'll start with James" she said. "Nooooooooooo!" Sunstorm screamed as he stepped back and drew a sword. The crowd backed up and arms and evilsheep also took out their swords. "No, I mean turn around! Behind you!" Sunstorm shouted.

They span around and saw, with complete surprise, four men on horseback standing towards the entrance of the castle. They were at least 10ft tall but didn't seem real. They were grey in colour and were armed with long steel swords. Their dark horses looked unearthly as they breathed in the cold night air. Suddenly they began to charge forwards.

Shouting aloud, the king's subjects dispersed in different directions. Lynns looked around frantically as she ran towards the side. She had to find TMK. Reaching a number of stone columns, she hid behind one whilst she scanned the courtyard for the person she wanted to protect.

The centre of the courtyard was a flurry of action. arms and SittingDuck were battling one warrior, whilst evilsheep was attempting to dodge the sword swipes from two. TMK and Sunstorm were tackling the other. Lynns caught sight of this battle and grabbed onto her crucifix. But it wasn't there. She looked down at her robes and could not find the golden chain. She was then pulled backwards as she realised where the chain had gone - it was now around her neck. The traitor grabbed onto both ends and pulled as hard as he could. Lynns kicked and grabbed with her arms as she struggled to breathe. A few moments later and her body went limp. The traitor released the chain and the cleric fell to the ground. Her face had turned a slight shade of purple as all the oxygen had been starved from it. The life had disappeared from her eyes.A shattering sound erupted through the courtyard as TMK and Sunstorm cheered. They had managed to make the horse they were attacking lose balance and fall to the ground, taking its warrior with them. As it made impact, the stone exploded into a thousand pieces showering everywhere. Sunstorm bent down to pick up some of the pieces and then coughed violently into his hand. Blood was spewing out of his mouth. He looked down at his chest and found the tip of TMK's sword. "Die traitor!" TMK said as he released the sword. "You've got it all wrong" Sunstorm managed to splutter as he fell forwards."Hahaha, oh yes! All wrong!" came a voice from behind TMK. He span around and saw Ilfirin holding a sword in one hand and Lynns' golden crucifix in the other. "WTF?!" TMK said as he realised what he had done. Angrily, he picked up his sword and began to duel the final traitor.

Shouts echoed through other parts of the courtyard as evilsheep had been thrown to the ground. The warrior trotted over towards him and lifted his sword ready to deal the final blow. At that moment, a shot of blue light sped across the yard, whooshing past TMK's swordfight and the other two subjects. The beam was directly at the centre of the warrior and resulted in a great crashing sound as the stone attacker was smashed into smitherines.

SittingDuck, arms and their own attacker stopped their fight to see what had caused the awesome blow.

At the foot of the courtyard, stood a wrinkly old man with hair as white as snow and eyes as blue as the sea. He had both of his arms outstretched with a single finger pointed at the two remaining warriors. Further blue beams shot out at supersonic speeds caused even more debris to fly through the air as they obliterated them.

Pleased at his work, he had one more job to do. He moved to face the final remaining battle - but this one had already been won. A figure had slumped to the ground gripping the wound in his stomach where the sword had entered. TMK hit the ground in pain as his attacker back away slowly. With glazed over blue eyes, stared directly at Ilfirin who had now moved into a sprint, attempting to run away from the Oracle's stare. It was no use. The shot of blue light was too fast as it struck him in the back, killing him instantly.TMK lay on the floor breathing his last gasps of air when a glow of white light omitted around him. His face began to change shape and morph into something else. SittingDuck, who was watching from metres away realised what had happened and immediately ran forward towards TMK. "Nooooooooooo! Father!" he shouted with immense shock. arms and evilsheep followed slowly.

As SittingDuck bent down in front of his dying father, a white glow started omitting from him also. His appearance altered dramatically to reveal an image of true royalty - the Prince of Gallador.

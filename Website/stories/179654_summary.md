### Chapter 1: The Gathering at Witchcraft Heights
The quiet, technological hum of Salem was shattered when EvilRunt was found atop the heights—his heart excised, his tongue silenced. The town, initially skeptical of the old superstitions, found themselves thrust into a nightmare. As the local publican recounted the grizzly discovery, the townspeople gathered at Gallows Hill, their fear morphing rapidly into paranoia. They knew not who walked among them, but they knew the Coven had returned.

### Chapter 2: The Shadow of the Hundredth
While the town bickered, a dark force stirred. The Serial Killer, a man obsessed with his macabre tally of deaths, set his sights on HydroP. Yet, fate intervened; the local Doctor, an army veteran with a penchant for poker, arrived just in time to patch the victim’s chest, saving him from the killer’s blade. However, the Coven was not so easily thwarted. Rebecca Nurse led a brutal strike against Arby, dragging him to a mechanical doom behind his own truck. By dawn, the first victim of the Witches was claimed, and the town’s panic reached a fever pitch.

### Chapter 3: Web of Deceit
The town’s first trial was a chaotic mess of accusations. The_Unknown (TU) was thrust upon the stage, pleading his innocence as a mere "only child," yet the mob’s bloodlust proved insatiable. He was strung up, his pockets searched for charms, only to yield a stick of gum and a magazine. The town had murdered an innocent. Meanwhile, the Serial Killer, stung by his failed strike, turned his rage toward I Like Pie—revealed to be the witch Rebecca Nurse. He slaughtered her in a cauldron, claiming his 100th kill. Simultaneously, the Coven struck at the fraudulent Doctor RisingDown, ending his charade in a gruesome display of retribution.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
As the numbers dwindled, the conflict tightened. Dangerous (Sarah Wilde) was unmasked and lynched by the angry mob, but the remaining witches, Blizzard and Xara, operated from the shadows. The Serial Killer, having finally claimed HydroP and the cop KT, found himself losing his edge. In a final, climactic confrontation, the last remaining townie, TBO, engaged the witches in a desperate, fire-fueled shootout. He felled the Reverend, but the odds were insurmountable. Struck by divine—or diabolical—intervention, TBO’s escape ended in a mangled wreck. The witches had won, their vengeance complete, leaving Salem a ghost town of blood and ash.

## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **[TI] arsbury***

Mafia's back for a 19th time and we're downsizing!! Damn credit crunch......

As usual, the following are the list of characters. These will be allocated to players who sign up randomly using the Excel rand() function!5 Mobsters (including a mafia elected Godfather)1 Serial Killer1 Sheriff1 Doctor8 Townies=> 16 playersOnly the godfather and mafia will know who the other mobsters are. Everyone else will be in the dark.NIGHT PHASE
- WHO DOES WHAT?

Each phase of the game begins with the night. The following can happen during this time:- Before the end of the first night, the mafia must elect their godfather. The godfather is the leader of the mafia. He, together with the other mobsters, will decide on person to kill each night. He will also choose one of them to conduct the kill. The godfather has the final say in any mafia night decision. He will also appear to be innocent to the sheriff's investigations and is immune to being killed at night.
- The serial killer is a madman who kills one person every night. Like the Godfather, he is immune to being killed/cured at night and will only die if lynched.
- The town sheriff has the power to investigate one town resident per night. By daytime, he will discover this person's role. He can do what he likes with this information but revealing it might reveal his identity.
- The doctor has the ability to save one person per night. That person will be protected from any possible attack by the mafia or serial killer. The doctor is forbidden from choosing the same person two nights in a row and he cannot choose himself.DAY PHASE
- LYNCHINGDuring the day phase, everyone in the town will find out who has been murdered. All townsfolk (including the mafia, the serial killer, doctor and sheriff) can then vote who they think are mobsters. This is the lynching process. There will be a deadline to which the people must vote by. If there is a draw, the deadline will be extended and only those drawing will be voted on. If there is still a draw by the new deadline, there will be no lynching. Once the result is in, the person will then be dead and their role will be revealed. Then that round is over and night begins again when the night roles make their next choices.WHO WINS?

The godfather and his mafia will win if they manage to kill everyone else off. The serial killer wins if he is the last person alive and the rest win if they manage to kill off all the mafia and kill the serial killer.

Everyone is allowed to post in the thread to discuss who they think is infected or try to make themselves less suspicious (if they are mafia!). You may NOT quote any part of messages sent by the game moderators. Doing so will result in your sudden death. Also, when you die you are dead and are no longer part of the game, you are not allowed to discuss the game with anyone.

Helping with the modding this game is Elrohir and Eltara! Hurrah!SIGN UPFor those who want to play, please ingame Ruffles in #1735 with a message saying who you are and that you want to play mafia. Deadline to do this is 22:00 GMT on Thursday 26th March.

Due to the limited number of players, if we have more than 16 people apply, the game moderators will select the most active players based on previous experience and current IRC activity. Once the players have been chosen, they will be posted here then role descriptions will be sent out shortly after and we'll begin!!

You cannot sign up if you are forum banned.

Good luck!

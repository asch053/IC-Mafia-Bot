## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **Jets***

In New York City

Pw3xqpw.jpg1500×1000

A number of recent graduates from university decided to start their new life. They would have to choose their line of work and do their best to survive the city that never sleeps. Some were able to find their dream jobs while others had to settle for something a little less than ideal. All of the recent graduates had hopes and dreams when they first arrived in New York City.

After the first night Several of the graduates found out that they would struggle waiting on their dream job, and decided to just pick up anything that would keep them from being homeless.

Making money was more important to some, than working their dream job, so being a Escort or a nun was all the same. The nun wasn’t really working in the sisterhood ,but that is what she told her mom as she wouldn’t approve of a life stripping.

The Actor is really a Waiter ,but nightly he will go out and impersonate someone has to always keep those acting skills sharp ya know.

Around 5 of the recent graduates answered a craiglist ad for employment. I guess they were pretty despite and this was easier to do so they thought. They all walked into one of those .99 pizza slices places and made their way to the back. A gentleman was sitting there dressed in a nice suit and told them they could start training today. All they had to do was choose from a list and let him know what they wanted to do.

A man around 50 that didn’t smell very good been watching these young ones come to the city and it brought back so many memories of how he lost his job ten years ago. He wanted revenge on younger ones that would work for less. “I was a skilled and valuable employee, I deserved a raise” Instead they fired him and hired someone just out of college.

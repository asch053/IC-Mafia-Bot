### Chapter 1: The Hollow Warehouse
Newcastle lay under the suffocating boot of "The Authority," their armored tanks patrolling streets once vibrant with human life. The Uprising, led by the brave Primo, sought a turning point in an abandoned warehouse. Instead of weapons, they found a digital graveyard of servers—a chilling map of an infiltration plot. Primo’s discovery cost him his life; his gruesome remains left as a warning to those who would dare defy their alien overlords. The Home Force, human collaborators acting as the Authority’s puppets, had already begun their systematic purge.

### Chapter 2: Blood in the Barracks
The carnage began in earnest during the first night. The local physician, Mr. Blonde, fell to a psychotic veteran—the Serial Killer—who staked him to the ground with clinical cruelty. Simultaneously, the Home Force dragged Lethal into the dark, executing him with electrified precision. By the time the sun rose, the Uprising was leaderless and terrified. Desperation took hold; they turned their suspicion toward Ordos, a simple-minded soul found in the barracks, and summarily executed him. The Collaborators struck again, silencing Dr. Harry Cox, leaving the Uprising without medical salvation.

### Chapter 3: The Web Unravels
As the death toll mounted, the internal rot became visible. KT, a collaborator, met a poetic end at the hands of the veteran, who poisoned her and silenced her forever. In retaliation, the Uprising caught wind of the traitor Dukey, who was discovered desecrating the corpse of the fallen Tishxo. A swift bullet ended his perverse displays. The power struggle shifted when the Uprising finally identified Nai, a former decorated cop turned collaborator, and tore him apart between two trucks—a brutal message that the rebellion had found its teeth.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
The war of attrition claimed the heroes one by one: the brave investigator Melvin, the veteran herself (Goddess of the Dead, who took her own life rather than surrender), and eventually, the last of the key insurgent leaders. By the final phase, only Zeraph remained against the remnants of the Home Force. In a desperate raid on the Authority’s headquarters, Zeraph hacked the alien mainframe, locking their suits into tombs. He dispatched the final collaborator, Rizzy, in a frantic, hand-to-hand struggle. Humanity was liberated from its silicon cage, though the cost of Newcastle’s freedom was written in the blood of the many who fell before the dawn.

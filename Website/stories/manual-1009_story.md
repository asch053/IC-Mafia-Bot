Welcome to Eyecee

The quaint town of Eyecee was beginning to boom. Corporations had taken notice and started setting up shop in hopes they would beat out any competition. The once small town is still adjusting to the sudden overgrowth of businesses and people. Family owned stores and restaurants that were once the heart of the town were now feeling the pressure. What could they do? The second those big corporations caught a whiff of their home they knew they had no chance.

Tirfy (town), a small family owned grocery store that had served its community for the past 50 years became the hub for disgruntled townies. The owners were concerned about their well-being and of the other small businesses they had become so close to over the years. They are taking a stand. Down with big corp!

Zarish (mob) big box store that has made its mark with huge supercenters, you can get everything you need in one place. Clothes, groceries, home items, a haircut, whatever you need or want. You name it, they have it. They are always looking to expand and only care about their bottom line. Business is business, no room for the little guy or any heart. Eyecee is the perfect place for them to grow. The town has been sheltered until now, time to bring them the wonders of the big box store.

ZaXo (Serial Killer) is a home-grown medium size business looking to expand. They set their sights on Eyecee because their roots were close by and they know the town has great potential. The owners of Zaxo are willing to do whatever it takes to stake their claim and expand.

With a town full of people willing to do whatever it takes to pad their bottom line, succeed, and survive who will come out the winner?

## 📜 Opening Briefing & Lore
*Original opening by Moderator **[TI] arsbury***

Ho ho ho!T'is the season to be jolly and also the season to murder some elves! Yes, it's Christmas and IC Mafia is back!As usual, this is the list of characters but since I have no idea how many people are going to sign up I haven't assigned any numbers yet. Unlike other games where I've cancelled due to low player numbers, if we get 10 or more we'll play!The following roles will be split and allocated randomly to those who have signed up:3 x Naughty Elves (including the elected Grinch)1 x Santa1 x Mrs Claus1 x Scrooge1 x Jack Frost (pro-town secret role)1 x Frosty the Snowman (alliance unknown secret role)4 x Nice ElvesOnly the Grinch and naughty elves will know who the other naughty elves are. Everyone else will be in the dark. --- NIGHT PHASE - WHO DOES WHAT? --- Each phase of the game begins with the night. The following can happen during this time:- The Grinch, together with his naughty elves, will decide one elf to kill. He will also choose which one of them will conduct the kill.- The Grinch is the elected leader of the naughty elves. He therefore has final say in any mafia night kill decision. He will also appear to be innocent to Santa's Little Helper investigations and is immune to being killed at night.- Scrooge is a serial killer who chooses one person to kill per night. He himself can be killed at night.- Mrs Claus can choose one person to save per night. If that person is chosen for death by the naughty elves or Scrooge, then they will be saved. Mrs Claus cannot choose the same person two nights in a row and cannot choose herself. She will also not heal Scrooge if he's chosen.- Santa acts like a judge in the event of a lynching vote tie. His role is allocated in addition to his normal role and therefore his alliance is unknown. He will have to choose who will be lynched in the event of a voting tie.- Frosty the snowman is a secret role. His powers and allegiance are both unknown!- Jack Frost is a secret role. He is aligned with the town but his powers are secret! --- DAY PHASE - LYNCHING --- During the day phase, everyone in Lapland will find out who has been murdered. All elves (including the naughty elves, the secret roles, the Grinch, Scrooge, Mrs Claus etc) can then vote who they think are the naughty elves. This is the lynching process. There will be a deadline to which the people must vote by. If there is a draw, the deadline will be extended and only those drawing will be voted on. If there is still a draw by the new deadline, there will be no lynching. Once the result is in, the person will then be dead and their role will be revealed. Then that round is over and night begins again when the night roles make their next choices. --- WHO WINS? --- The Grinch and his naughty elves will win if they manage to kill everyone else off. Scrooge wins if he is the last person alive and the rest win if they manage to kill off all the naughty elves and Scrooge. --- RULES --- Everyone is allowed to post in the thread to discuss who they think is naughty or try to make themselves less suspicious (if they are naughty!). You may NOT quote any part of messages sent by the game moderators. Doing so will result in your sudden death. Also, when you die you are dead and are no longer part of the game, you are not allowed to discuss the game with anyone.Finally, you MUST be IRC active and hang around in #mafia.m --- FINAL PLAYER LIST AND ROLES --- 1. --- BUZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZZ!The annoying sound of the alarm clock rang throughout the creaky cabin as the battered old body of Scrooge stirred in his bed. He wrinkled up his face and muttered some obscenities as he swung his arm around to put an end to the dreadful sound."Meh, wtf is the time?" the old man asked himself as he slowly peeled open his eyelids. The fuzziness of sleep slowly disappeared and he fixated his gaze on the time. "Meh, too early... just a few more minutes" he said closing his eyes again.A few moments later, the ringing of the clock was replaced with a gentle snore. --- "Rise and shine kids!" Ginger Jollybells shouted whilst clapping her hands. "It's the holidays! No staying in bed so late!"Holly Jollybells heard the sound of her mother's voice, immediately jumped out of bed and peered out of the window. It was early and her friends were already out playing in the freshly laid snow! She bent down and slipped on her red and green velvet shoes, the golden bells on the tips jingling as she did so."Morning mum! I'm going out to play!" she said fleetingly to her mother as she made her way to the door."No problem, be back for breakfast though. I'm making pancakes!" Ginger replied.Holly grinned and opened the front door. A cool gust of wind greeted her but her attention was quickly caught by the bright red parcel on the doorstep.Wow an early Christmas present! She thought to herself as she picked it up. It was wrapped in a golden ribbon and was accompanied by a purple tag. She took a hand and flipped over the tag to reveal the words: "To the Jollybells, Merry Christmas! Open me!!"Jumping with glee, Holly quickly untied the ribbon and opened the box....The sound of her daughter's scream made Ginger drop the pancake mix on the floor and dash hurriedly to the front door. She found her daughter crying pointing to a red parcel on the ground."What's the matter? What is that?" she asked as she peered inside the present. The dull black eyes of Frosty the Snowman stared back at her from his decapitated head.The scream of Ginger Jollybells which echoed across Lapland was something the village had not heard for years. Their mascot, Gwynedd, was dead. --- It is the first --- DAY --- phase!All Lapland residents have until 12:00 (midday) UK time on Friday to cast their votes on who you want to lynch!Remember you're allowed to change your votes, but do not edit your post, make a new one!To clarify, Frosty the Snowman was the alliance unknown secret role. --- It had just started to get dark and xara was standing at the giant christmas tree admiring the glimmer of the bright lights. It had always amazed her how everyone got together at Christmas. A small tear formed in the corner of her eye and trickled down her cheek. Lapland had lynched Santa Claus and this year's Christmas was going to be thrown into turmoil as a result. She closed her eyes as the imagined the millions of children around the world waking up in the morning without any presents."Now now dear, no need to cry" came a croaky voice behind her.xara span around to find she was joined by a wrinkled old man. He was dressed in a tattered brown suit and bent over a walking stick. "It all seems to be falling apart! she sobbed."I know, I'm the happiest I've felt in years" Scrooge said as he lifted his head a little.xara only had time to glance towards him in surprise as he plunged a knife directly into her back. She screamed out in pain and lurched forward towards the tree, desperately trying to get away from the crazy demented man. He cackled in response as he watched the innocent nice elf flail helplessly in front of him.KT and Wendy watched this assault from behind the christmas tree with added interest. They had orders from the Grinch to take out Jets immediately but after witnessing him approach xara and then withdraw his knife, they were keen to see how the drama unfolded. KT then gave the nod to Wendy who proceeded with the previously arranged plan.Jets continued watching as xara grabbed onto the tree's branches in an attempt to escape from him. He gripped onto the knife in his hand and followed her but looked up when he heard the rustling of branches and the snapping of wood. He dropped the knife in a frantic move to turn around with his walking stick and get away in the opposite direction as the giant christmas tree started to crash down. xara also peered towards the tree and had no chance as a huge crashing sound echoed throughout Lapland. In a flash, the great landmark which stoof for all things christmas had suddently been removed. Amonst the rubble of fairy lights and broken tree decorations, lay the crushed bodies of xara and Jets.Meanwhile the Grinch was following his own carefully executed plan.As one of the only town residents alive, Alchoran rushed out of his cabin to investigate where the loud crashing has originated from. Genesis smashed another bottle on the side of the cabin and smiled as he walked towards Alch with the jagged glass outstretched.A moment later and the three surviving naughty elves gathered at fallen christmas tree. Dead bodies were littered around the village square and blood had tainted the pure white snow to an evil red colour. Genesis, the Grinch, threw his bloody broken bottle to the ground and highfived KT and Wendy in celebration!The blue, green and gold christmas lights surrounding the tree flickered as they lost their power supply and switched off, plunging the square into and eerie darkness.

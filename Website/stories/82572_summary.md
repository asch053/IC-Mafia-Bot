### Chapter 1: The Gathering at Rae
The portal flared a malevolent blue, depositing a new cohort of prisoners onto the desolate, hellish surface of Rae. These were the dregs of the Arsylian galaxy: thieves, murderers, and the whispered-about members of the Arsylian Mafia. As the newcomers were stripped of their dignity and possessions, a grim realization settled over the camp. The anarchy of Rae was no longer enough; the convicts, fearing a shadow-enslavement by the Mob, turned to the makeshift court. They began to vote, casting their peers into the void in a desperate, blind attempt to purge the organized rot from their ranks.

### Chapter 2: First Blood
The silence of the kangaroo-dung hovels was shattered by the sound of splintering wood. Torqez, a simple poacher, was the first to fall—crushed beneath his own home by the ruthless hands of the Arsylian Mafia. But the Mob was not the only reaper on Rae. Arfeh, a loud-mouthed agitator, found himself ensnared in a nightmare of honey and swarming, ravenous rats—the signature, sadistic hallmark of the lone Serial Killer. The morning sun rose over a double grave, and the barbecue that followed was tainted by the metallic taste of fear.

### Chapter 3: The Mirror-Eater’s Fall
Suspicion turned toward the eccentric, and eyes fell upon Melvin, a self-proclaimed mirror-eater whose desperate attempts to divert blame onto others only sealed his fate. In a display of raw prison brutality, the convicts took the law into their own hands. Following a chorus of crude suggestions, Nolio led the charge, and Melvin’s life was extinguished in the dust. The court cheered, yet as the body cooled, the whispers persisted: the "justice" of the crowd had claimed yet another innocent soul, leaving the true predators to walk free.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
As the night descended again, the carnage intensified. Shlimy, the simple farmboy, met his end by piano wire, while Elbekko, the digital hermit, suffered a gruesome crucifixion at the hands of the Serial Killer. The prisoners awoke to find a landscape littered with corpses—both human and bovine. With the toll of casualties rising and the Mafia’s grip tightening, the convicts faced the ultimate question: had they already lost the battle for their own survival, or could they find the true wolves hiding among the sheep before the portal closed on them forever?

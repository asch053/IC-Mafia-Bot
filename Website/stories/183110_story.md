*Original opening by Moderator **Gwynedd***

--
- Welcome to Mafia 41. See if you can survive the Chicago Outfit during prohibition, you booze hounds! --
- The storyProhibition had been good for Al Capone and his family. Thanks to grog running Capone was making $100 million per year, a princely sum. His family were there to support him; Bob Calandra, Vince DeLuca, Frankie La Porte, Ross Prio and Frank Nitti.

Capone called a meeting of the Chicago Outfit. It was time to press these irishmen for all they were worth. Capone looked to his second, Frank Nitti. 'One day, you will inherit all of this from me. One day you will lead mi familia. You are a brother to me.'Capone looked around the table, making eye contact with each member of his famiy. The time was come. 'Mi familia, I have a problem. These irish, they do not show the proper respect. They buy their alcohol from others. They do not understand, we run this town. What should we do about this.'Nitti stood up. 'I would kill for you in an instant, any time. We should destroy these irishmen, make them fear us. They will pay proper respect or they will pay with their lives!' The rest of the family started clapping at this speech.

Capone lifted his hand up and everyone went silent. Capone looked at Nitti, 'Your plan intrigues me. Perhaps if we did kill a few they would learn. We start now.' Capone turned to Bob Calandra. 'Bob, your time has come to show your love for the family. You need to show these irish that we will not accept this. We run this town, not them.'Calandra nodded. He knew what was to be done. Following the meeting Calandra drove down the street when he located a bum sitting on the corner. This homeless fool would do. Flint looked up into the eyes of Calandra. Calandra drew his pistol, shooting Flint square between the eyes. Calandra pulled out his knife and cut off Flint's head. Calandra proceeded to use Flint's blood to write on the wall a message to the town. 'The Chicago Outfit run this town, respect us or die'.

The next morning a young irishman was walking along the street when he saw Flint slumped in the gutter. He didn't think much of this, it was fairly normal for Flint to be sleeping in the gutter. As he approached, the irishman noted that Flint seemed smaller somehow. He looked up and saw Flint's head piked on a nearby fence. He read the message written above the head. The town had something to fear.A meeting was called, but the mayor was out of town, he would not be returning until the following day. Today was a day to mourn, tomorrow would be the day to come together as a town and decide what to do.

Meanwhile, the irishman was enraged by the blood he had seen. It reminded him of another time, another place. He remembered the metallic smell, the taste of his own sweat and his victim's despair. It was time to start killing again, but this time he would just be killing for killing's sake. --

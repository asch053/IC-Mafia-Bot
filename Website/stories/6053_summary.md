### Chapter 1: The Bloody Dawn
Ciaculli was a town suffocated by the shadow of the Greco crime family. The silence was shattered when Primo was found gutted in his shop, a gruesome signature of the encroaching syndicate. As Amok—Primo’s lover—reeled in horror, the “little bird” Ciaschiteddu silenced him with a garrote. The Pope’s reach was absolute, and the town plunged into a cycle of terror. Yet, amid the mafia’s precision, a darker, chaotic pulse beat in the shadows: the Serial Killer had arrived, hungry for the visceral power of the kill.

### Chapter 2: The House of Ashes
The town’s first attempt at justice ended in catastrophic failure. They targeted EvilRunt, a strange, lonely soul whose house became his tomb. His demise triggered a gas explosion that claimed not only his life but the life of Translucent, one of the town’s few remaining defenders. With the police force crippled in a single day, the town descended into paranoid bedlam. A frantic, chauvinistic lynch eventually took the life of Missy, but the Greco family remained firmly in control.

### Chapter 3: The Mill and the Pyre
As the body count rose, the town’s desperation grew. HydroP, pleading his innocence until his final breath, was chased through the town before being ground into the stones of the Giants Flour Mill—a brutal end for a townie caught in the crossfire. The hypocrisy of the Greco family was exposed when the town discovered Ciaschiteddu (Luker) and the townie Ordos in a secret, illicit tryst. The town exacted swift, fiery retribution, burning them alive within their shared home.

### Chapter 4: The Fall of the Pope
The endgame approached with a feverish intensity. The town turned their ire toward Salvatore "The Senator" Greco, whose gruesome death by being dragged behind two cars left the remaining Grecos isolated. Finally, the patriarch himself, Michele "The Pope" Greco, sat in his study with a revolver. Finding the chamber empty, he was dragged to the gantry by the vengeful townsfolk. He died in a wretched display of public humiliation, defecating in terror as the rope tightened.

### Chapter 5: The Final Stagnation
With the Greco family dismantled, the town was not saved—it was hollow. The final survivors—the Doctor, the Serial Killer, a townie, and the last remaining Greco—descended into a final, depraved orgy of madness. In a tragic, dark irony, the Doctor laced the party’s refreshments with cyanide. As the survivors foamed at the mouth, gasping for air while the smell of almonds filled the room, the final silence fell over Ciaculli. No one was left to rule the ruins.

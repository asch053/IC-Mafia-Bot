### Chapter 1: The Gathering of Shadows
The peaceful facade of Elmsville shattered under the weight of a ringing phone. Kristy’s boredom was replaced by cold terror, a prelude to a massacre that would leave her and Ron gutted, their bodies serving as grim ornaments on the town’s front lawn. As news vans swarmed Elmsville High, the survivors—Sidney, Gale, and the rest—realized that the horror wasn't just a movie trope; it was a lethal, unfolding reality.

### Chapter 2: The Curse of the Tape
While the town panicked, calculating the motives of the hidden "psychos" and the silent serial killers, a sinister trap was sprung. Jimmy "Jigsaw," a man who lived by riddles, found himself caught in a different kind of game. A mysterious text led him to a cursed video—a digital death sentence from Samara Morgan. The town watched in horror as Jimmy, entranced by the flickering static of his own heart monitor, was pulled into the abyss by the vengeful spirit. His disappearance left the town’s defenses crippled, signaling that the Mob's reach extended far beyond simple knives and masks.

### Chapter 3: The Unfinished Requiem
The archive falls silent after Jigsaw’s tragic demise. With the town’s collective investigation stalled and the killers shifting through the shadows of the night, the battle for Elmsville remains a haunting cliffhanger. The threads of the investigation were cut short, leaving the fate of Sidney Prescott and the remaining inhabitants of this doomed town suspended in the amber of an incomplete record.

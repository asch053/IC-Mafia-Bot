### Chapter 1: The Gathering
Flight 0022 was meant to be a marvel of human ingenuity, a smooth, turbulence-free passage to the Luxos Capital. But within the luxurious cabins, a darker agenda was brewing. Among the 22 passengers, an insurgency lay dormant, waiting for the solar winds to provide the perfect cover. As the ship shook, a hidden suitcase burst open to reveal weaponry, and the serenity of the flight was shattered by the cold-blooded execution of the cockpit crew. The ship was now a tomb, drifting aimlessly through the void.

### Chapter 2: First Blood
Chaos reigned as the passengers realized their pilots were dead. In the vacuum of leadership, paranoia took root. The town attempted to organize, electing Genesis as their Captain, but their efforts were hampered by the shadows stalking the corridors. The Serial Killer, a rogue element in the madness, claimed their first victim, while the Mafia, led by an unknown Godfather, systematically hunted those who posed a threat to their hostile takeover.

### Chapter 3: Web of Deceit
The passenger list grew shorter with every passing day. The Doctor (ARFeh) and the Cops tried to hold the line, investigating whispers and pinning the blame on suspected conspirators. However, the Mafia’s reach was absolute. They silenced the Cops, removed the town's protectors, and masterfully manipulated the lynch votes to dispose of anyone standing in their way—such as the unfortunate Anarchy, who was sacrificed to their machinations.

### Chapter 4: The Final Stand
As the ship’s oxygen levels dwindled and the end grew near, the surviving conspirators made their move. Thirdrock, the orchestrator, ended the charade by descending upon Captain Genesis in his office, silencing the last beacon of hope. With ruthless efficiency, Melvin, Amok, and Lexuzis turned on the remaining passengers. As the final cries of the innocent faded into the cold silence of space, the Mafia stood triumphant among the wreckage—the true masters of Flight 0022.

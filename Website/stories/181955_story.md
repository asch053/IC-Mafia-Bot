They are all known to have dodged the law at some point.Sheriff John Behan is trying to get a handle on things before they spin even further out of control, but he has recently begun receiving mysterious money packed envelopes during the night that contain simple two word commands. If he is lynched, the investigations die with him.John Henry "Doc" Holliday - The pro-town "Doctor" who couldn't hack it as a dentist, tries to save someone each night with his bloody pliers and bottle of whiskey. He has a horrible temper and if the town moves to lynch him, he will pull his derringer and shoot the person closest to him (whomever he thinks that may be) before being subdued...If attacked at night, he will take his killer with him into death. ### 🌙 Night / Day Narrative Scene (02-Mar-2013 15:03:27)

Sheriff Behan sat and stared at the money filled envelope sitting on the desk in front of him. He reached into his bottom desk drawer and pulled out a bottle of whiskey, pouring 3 fingers of it into a glass tumbler. He corked the bottle and set it on the desk, growing increasingly irritated and angry at the words contained within the letter. He reached over and turned up the wick on the lamp next to him so that he could peer closely at the writing. Suddenly he jerked his head up and stared out the window at the saloon across the street. He had recognized the badly misspelled handwriting and knew who had penned the letter. He leaned back in his chair, pulling his pistol and laying it gently on the desk. He pulled the lever on it, carefully removed the cylinder and checked the wax seal on each round to make sure they weren't compromised. After several minutes deep in thought, he reassembled the pistol and decisively returned it to his waistband. He turned to the untouched whiskey and picked it up. He peered at the amber liquid inside, mesmerized as it caught and refracted the light from his lamp. In a single motion he leaned his head back and tossed the burning liquid to the back of his mouth and swallowed it with a brief grimace.He stepped out of his office, turned to his left and walked 5 paces to the corner of the building and stepped back against the wall, concealing himself within the shadow of the awning. It was only a quarter-moon tonight but he wasn't taking any chances. After a bit, Curly Bill came out through the front doors of the saloon, turned down the alley and began urinating against the building. Behan pulled the pistol from his waistband, cocked it behind his back, thrust his other hand into his vest pocket and silently crossed the street. Curly Bill was having trouble standing even with one hand against the wall...urine splashing his trousers as well as his shoes and the wall. Behan increased his stride, pulling the pistol to his front. When he came within 10 feet of the drunk he raised the pistol, extended his arm and fired a single shot into the right side of Curly Bill's head. Without slowing he continued down the alley, turned the left corner and entered the O.K. Corral. He swiftly readied his horse and rode out to the Outlaw camp. As he came within hearing range of the fire he called out,"'Lo there the fire!""Who's there and whadda ya' want!", Billy Claiborne snapped."I'm here to talk business...", Behan smiled as he dismounted, hobbled his horse and approached the fire.Curly Bill Brocius (RisingDown) is now dead, and Sheriff Johnny Behan has secretly joined the Outlaws as its new GodFather.(NB: I jumped the gun on RisingDown, thinking he was too busy to play.

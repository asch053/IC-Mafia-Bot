📖 Imperial Conflict Mafia Codex

Welcome to the comprehensive rulebook and game guide.

Game Modes

Classic Mafia

The traditional game of social deduction. The Town must root out the informed minority (Mafia) and any Neutral killers before they lose their voting majority.

Battle Royale

A fast-paced bloodbath where everyone is a Vigilante. There are no daytime lynches—only night kills. Last person standing wins.

The P.E.U. Skill Score Formula

The Skill Score is a "Moneyball"-style advanced metric to determine a player's overall value, combining three core stats:

  • (P) Persuasion: Voting with the majority to successfully lynch a target.
  • (E) Elusiveness: Avoiding getting killed at night or lynched during the day.
  • (U) Understanding: Voting against opposing factions and avoiding voting for your own team.

🏛️ Classic Mafia Mode (5–19 Players)

Asymmetric Social Deduction

An uninformed majority (Town) battles an informed syndicate (Mafia) and solitary third-party killers (Serial Killer / Jester). Alternates between Night Phases (secret DM abilities resolved in strict priority order) and Day Phases (public deliberation and `/vote` lynches).

Win Condition Evaluation Order:
1️⃣ Serial Killer Solo Win: SK is the last player alive (or in an unbreakable 1v1 stalemate).
2️⃣ Town Victory: All Mafia and hostile Neutrals (SK) are eliminated.
3️⃣ Mafia Victory: Mafia count ≥ Living Town count (and no living Serial Killer remains).

⚔️ Battle Royale Mode (Free-For-All)

Every Player For Themselves

A high-octane nocturnal bloodbath where 100% of players spawn as lone-wolf Vigilantes. Daytime voting phases are bypassed completely—the game cycles continuously through Night combat phases until only one survivor remains.

Vigilante Tactical Choice Each Night:
• Submit /kill <player> (Priority 3) to eliminate a rival, OR
• Submit /block <player> (Priority 1) to disrupt a rival's attack.
Only your last submitted DM command before the timer expires is executed!

🧮 The P.E.U. "Moneyball" Skill Score System (0.00 – 5.00 Scale)

Inspired by sabermetric "Moneyball" analytics, the Imperial Conflict Mafia Skill Score evaluates individual player quality independent of raw faction luck. Every player receives three normalized pillar ratings from 0.00 to 5.00—Persuasion (P), Elusiveness (E), and Understanding (U)—which combine into a composite rating out of 5.00.

Composite Skill Score = (W_P × Persuasion) + (W_E × Elusiveness) + (W_U × Understanding)   |   Default Weights: W_P = 0.33, W_E = 0.33, W_U = 0.34

🗣️ (P) Persuasion

Weight: 33.3%

Measures your ability to drive town consensus and land on the successful daytime lynch wagon without indecisive flip-flopping.

Phase Weight: p_num = 2 × Day#
Decisiveness = 1 - (VoteSwitches / TotalVotesInPhase)
P_game = [ Σ (p_num × OnWinningLynch × Decisiveness) / Σ p_num ] × 5.0
  • Late-Game Amplification: Day 3 lynches (weight 6) matter 3× as much as Day 1 lynches (weight 2).
  • Decisiveness Penalty: Switching votes repeatedly during the same Day reduces your Persuasion credit for that phase.

🛡️ (E) Elusiveness

Weight: 33.3%

Measures survival longevity using Triangular Phase Weighting. Surviving deep into a 10-phase marathon earns exponentially more credit than surviving Night 1.

Survived Weight = L × (L + 1) / 2
Total Game Weight = T × (T + 1) / 2
E_game = [ L(L + 1) / T(T + 1) ] × 5.0
  • Full Survival (L = T): Always awards a perfect 5.00 for that match.
  • Deep Run Credit: Dying on Phase 7 of an 8-phase game still yields 3.89 / 5.00 (`28 / 36 × 5`), whereas dying Night 1 yields 0.14.

🧠 (U) Understanding

Weight: 33.4%

Measures faction-balanced win conversion and strategic game sense, protected by an Early-Death Cutoff so Night 1 victims aren't punished.

Early-Death Cutoff = floor(0.25 × TotalPhases)
U = (0.33×Win%_Town + 0.33×Win%_Mafia + 0.34×Win%_Neutral) × 5.0
  • 25% Early-Death Protection: In Discord Bot telemetry (`skillscore_calc.py`), if you die within the first 25% of phases (`L ≤ floor(0.25 × T)`), that match is excluded from your Understanding denominator.
  • Scumhunting Vote Accuracy %: Tracks the % of a Town player's end-of-day lynch votes cast against actual Mafia members (`≥ 60%` unlocks 🎯 Deadshot Scumhunter).
